## Gate 2. End result (draft)
- After this subject you can: Before you call someone "narcissist" or "manipulative", check what you are actually looking at and name it from what they did.
- Real moment: Someone's behavior, seen or told to you, often in your own relationships.
- Real-world test: Given new descriptions of behavior, you say what it is (or that it is an ordinary bad day) and point to what shows it.
