## The rule (given before the first case)
Step 3 and Step 4 come before Rhonda (the first case) and before every scored item. In my words: find the act and the belief or self-picture it clashes with. If the mind eases the clash AFTER the act by an excuse, a jab at the source, "it doesn't matter", or the belief sliding with nothing new learned, it is dissonance. If the answer was picked first and the looking came after, it is motivated reasoning. If new evidence moved the belief, it is an honest change. If the person just fixed the act, it is ordinary. If neither signs nor an act are shown, there is too little to tell. Response: in yourself, say the contradiction and change the act or belief; in someone else, leave a face-saving way out.
