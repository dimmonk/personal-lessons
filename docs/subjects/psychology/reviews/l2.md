# Learner review, l2 "A mind talking itself into something" (check: 8 new, 7 of 8)

## Before
Hearing "she's rationalizing", I could say "making excuses" and little else. I could not tell excuse-after-the-fact from wanting-an-answer-first, or name sunk cost beyond "wasting money".

## The rule
Given before the first case (Steps 1 to 4, worked example in Step 5). In my words: ask what came first. Act first, then an excuse: dissonance. Answer first, then hunting for reasons: motivated reasoning. A belief held already, with a different standard for evidence on each side: confirmation bias. A reason that points back at spent money or time: sunk cost (wins if two fit). Evidence first, belief moves, and they can say what would move them back: honest change. A normal reason: ordinary. Too few facts: too little to tell.
