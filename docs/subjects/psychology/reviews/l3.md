## Before

## The rule
Given before the first case (Step 1 and Step 3, ahead of the worked case and all items). In my words: catch the thought in its own words, test it against the facts (evidence for and against, likeliest outcome, what I'd tell a friend, can I check). Facts don't back it: it is a trap (catastrophizing = worst outcome as certain; mind reading = sure of what someone thinks with no word from them; all-or-nothing = one slip becomes "total/always/never"), so let it go. Facts back it: fair worry, so make a plan. Normal feeling, no trap: ordinary, give it time and kindness. Name reached for but no thought or facts shown: too little to tell, find out more.
