## Before
On the real task I can spot the obvious ones (a gaslighter, "a narcissist" said after a bad date) but I mix up the near names (projection against mind reading, dissonance against motivated reasoning) and I would give a name to almost anything.

## The rule
Given before the first case: Steps 1 to 4 come first (method in order, every name with its signs and what to do, the limits, one worked example). In my words: ask what kind of thing it is (my own thinking, a move on someone, a lasting way someone is, or an ordinary reaction). Look for a hard time first, then count (how long, how many people). Name only if the signs show together. If the facts are thin, or the story only holds a label someone gave, say "too little to tell". Then the response that goes with the name. Yes, the rule precedes item 1.

## Stuck
Blockers: none that stop a beginner. Items that could cost points or trust:
1. Item 9 says "the mirror image of the last story". Items shuffle, so "the last story" may be another one. Fix: name item 8's facts or drop the reference.
2. Check 18 (Esperanza): "five weeks, two complaints". Q2 key is "The story does not say". "Once or twice, with no pattern" is just as natural. The drill never keys that option, and Step 1 never says what "does not say" means. Fix: one line in Step 1, for example "a few weeks, one side, no outside view: the story does not say", or a drill item that keys it.
3. Check 15 (own pattern): the key is "Get outside help". The borderline line says "keep firm limits", and the override ("the pattern is your own") sits in one line in Step 2. It is never practised before the check. A beginner could pick limits. Fix: a drill item with the override, or restate it in Step 1.
4. Item 8 (a coworker cried once, called "borderline"): keyed "too little to tell". "Ordinary" is arguable: one reaction, nothing to fix. The split rests on "we cannot see why she cried". Step 1's definition of ordinary ("facts show a normal reason") does settle it, but narrowly.
5. Item 7 (antisocial): "routine lies" and "no remorse" are not shown, only secrets used and amusement. A beginner gets there by elimination, not by the listed signs. Fix: add one lie, or change the signs line.
6. Item 5 (projection): "sure what someone thinks, with no word from them" also fits mind reading exactly. The tie-break (Evander's own job hunt) is only in the feedback, not in Step 2.
7. "Then count" in Step 1 does not say what to count (years, people).
8. 18 name options on a phone, on every story, scroll a lot. Nice-to-have: group them under the Q1 answer.
9. Slip ids and "Check question 10 to 19" numbering after "9 questions" may confuse.
