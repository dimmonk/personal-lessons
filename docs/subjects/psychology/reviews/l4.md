# Learner review: l4 Confidence or narcissism

Read as a beginner from docs/learner-view/psychology-l4.md only. Items 11 and 12 (sunk cost, catastrophizing) are the two earlier-lesson stories, taken as intended.

## Before
I would have called anyone who boasts "a narcissist". I had no test for telling confidence from narcissism.

## The rule (given before the first case)
Step 1, before the first A/B pair: ignore how sure someone sounds; test what happens to their regard for other people when their confidence is tested (criticism, a rival's win, being passed over). Regard holds = steady self-esteem; the other person must be cut down = narcissism. Step 2 gives the naming bar: the three main signs (grand picture, need for admiration, little empathy) together, for years, with many people. Fewer = traits. Step 3 gives the shapes (outward attack = grandiose, inward withdrawal = vulnerable). Step 4 gives healthy / traits / ordinary / too little to tell and what to do. The rule comes before the worked example (Step 5) and the first item.

## After
- Tell confidence from narcissism by what happens to regard for others under a test (items 1, 5, 13, 20).
- Refuse the word on fewer than three main signs or less than years (items 6, 16, 19: traits).
- Tell grandiose from vulnerable by whether it cracks outward or inward (items 2, 3, 9, 10, 14, 15).
- Say "too little to tell" and name what to watch for (items 7, 17).
