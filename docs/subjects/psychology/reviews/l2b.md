## The rule
Given before the first scored case (Step 4, and the pieces in Steps 2 and 3):
- Anchoring: a first number, even random or self-serving, pulls my guess; I move too little from it. Fair twin: a number backed by facts. Test: would my estimate be different if the first number had been different? Fix: make my own estimate first, name it first.
- Hindsight: once the ending is known it feels obvious. Fair twins: a prediction recorded beforehand, or criticism the facts of the day backed. Test: what could be known on the day? Fix: judge by what could be known then, write predictions down.
- Ordinary / too little to tell are defined in Step 1.
The rule comes before the worked example and every scored item. The Teo table (Step 2) comes before Step 4 but carries its own rule above it. Met.
