## Before
Could only say "that sounds like a narcissist" for any clingy, rude or dramatic person. Could not tell borderline, histrionic, narcissistic or antisocial apart.

## The rule
Given before the first case (Steps 1 to 4, ahead of the worked example in Step 5):
1. Check first whether a hard time (grief, stress, illness) explains it, and whether it is years and many people.
2. Ask what drives it: fear of being left (borderline), need to be noticed (histrionic), need to be admired as better (narcissistic: loud = grandiose, quiet and bitter = vulnerable), indifference to others (antisocial).
3. If the facts show a normal reason, it is ordinary. If the facts are too few, say too little to tell.
4. Unpleasant, rude or "toxic" is not a diagnosis. Look for signs against the name too.
