# Learner review: l6 "Moves made on someone"
Read only docs/learner-view/psychology-l6.md (rendered fresh, no mid-edit problem).

## Before
Could say "that sounds toxic" and little else. Could not tell gaslighting from a plain disagreement, or DARVO from changing the subject.

## The rule (given before the first case)
Step 1 gives it before any case: five questions, name last; a move is named by its whole sequence and what it does, never one uncomfortable moment. Ordinary = the facts show a normal reason; too little to tell = too few facts. Steps 3-6 add one sentence each (gaslighting: repeated, memory denied until self-doubt; DARVO: deny + attack + flip, in one exchange; love-bombing: speed beyond knowledge, then cold; projection: accuses another of what they show, no backing). Met.
