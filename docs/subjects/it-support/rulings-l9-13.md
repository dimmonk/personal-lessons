# IT Help Desk lessons 9 to 13: rulings on the first reviews (2026-10-23)

Reviews: `review-learner-l9-13.md`, `review-research-l9-13.md`. Every finding is fixed. `rulings-l1-8.md` still holds.

1. **The boot chain has one wording, lesson 9's**: power, start-up self-test, firmware (which finds the boot drive in the boot
   order), the boot drive, Windows loading, sign-in and profile. "Boot order" belongs to the firmware link everywhere; "no
   bootable device" is the firmware not finding a drive it can start from (never "the start-up test"). Lessons 10 to 13 use these
   words exactly.
2. **Wrong options are real novice moves, not only expensive ones.** Every question has at least one wrong option that is cheap
   but does not split (a test on the wrong link, a test that cannot tell the two sides apart, two changes at once), and some
   questions are right with a replacement or a bigger step because earlier tests in the ticket already pointed there. "Reinstall"
   and "order a new one" stop being automatic decoys.
3. **Phone calls**: the reply that continues is not always a question; sometimes the right turn is saying it back, setting a
   time, or stopping to confirm who the caller is.
4. **Recall questions** cannot be guessed from the newest or the obvious word: give options from the same family (DDR3, DDR4,
   DDR5 in a question where the older one is right; sockets of both makers).
5. **Each system lesson names its chain** (lesson 12: the signal path from the computer's port, through the cable and adapter, to
   the screen's input and panel; lesson 13: app, queue, driver, the connection from this computer, the printer, and inside a laser
   printer the seven steps of the laser process: processing, charging, exposing, developing, transferring, fusing, cleaning, with
   each print defect on its step).
6. **Facts**: RAID 0 cannot be rebuilt (restore from backup); DVI-D is digital only, DVI-I carries both, DVI-A analog; a USB stick
   with nothing to start is skipped by most firmware and does not by itself cause "no bootable device"; never two changes in one
   step (lesson 6). Anything the reviewer marked "verify" is checked against a published source before it stays.
7. **Coverage**: add the A+ sub-bullets the research review lists as uncovered to the lesson whose system they belong to.
