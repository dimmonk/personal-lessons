## Part 2: NEW BLOCKERS

**N1. L27, ruling 20 (a recurring clause marks wrong options).** Counted over the rendered options: a causal "since" or "because" tail is on about 10 of 51 wrong options and 1 of 17 right ones (about 20 percent against 6 percent). It is the same defect that blocked l22 and l23 (18 against 3 percent). Examples: "Wednesday night's backup alone, since it is the newest"; "Sunday's full alone, since it is the only full one"; "Pour the water on the strip, since it is a small fire"; "Thursday night's backup alone, since it is the newest one made"; "Leave it, because dust only matters for how the room looks"; "Put on an ESD strap first, since a battery box can hold a charge"; "An emergency change, because 40 PCs are involved"; "Not a change, because the PCs keep working during it"; "Dust: the shelves need cleaning, because dust causes the shocks"; "Sunday's full alone, because a full backup is the complete copy". A beginner learns that an option with a reason tail is the wrong one. Fix: remove the tails from wrong options (or give the right options and the other wrong ones the same tail in equal share).

**N2. L29, ruling 20, worse.** Causal "since" tail on about 9 of 48 wrong options and 0 of 16 right ones. Examples: "Add more memory to the PC, since a slow start means it runs short"; "Replace the motherboard, since a black screen with beeps means the board"; "Replace the power supply, since the fans spin but nothing shows"; "Replace the toner cartridge..., since the page never comes out"; "Restart the shared printer..., since a job is stuck"; "Order a new battery..., since it is two years old"; "Run the startup repair now, since it is meant to keep the files"; "Tell Essie it is fine to carry on, since the page closed..."; "Go ahead, since being careful is enough..."; "Reset the password... since the chat says it is Essie..." In a ten-ticket graded check this lets a learner pass by choosing the option with no reason. Fix: as for N1; the right options carry none today.

No other new blocker found: no wrong fact, no ruling 3, 5, 6, 7, 11, 13, 17 or 21 contradiction, no fact asked that was never taught, no question needing a fact the ticket lacks, in l18 to l29.
