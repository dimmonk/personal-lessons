# Learner review, singing l1 to l6 (cold beginner, learner views only)

Sung tries cannot be sung here. I judged instructions, feedback words and case questions. Written answers: I answered L1 and L3 case questions before reading the marks.

## L1 Your range: PASS (with 2 minor defects)
My own words: before blaming the ear, check the song's range. Sing the hard note softly. If it will not come even softly, the song is too high; if only air or a rumble, too low; if it comes easily, the range is fine and the problem is a later check. I could state this after one read.
Score on the check (answered blind): 5 of 5. Practice 6 of 6.
1. Item 8 (Mia, "even softly... only by straining"): "straining softly" blurs the soft test; "some do not come at all" saves it. Fix: drop "straining", say "some do not come out at all, even softly".
2. Steps 1 and 2 use "the line drawn", "sharp (a shade over)", "the next check" before anyone has met them. Sharp is only half explained. Fix: one plain sentence on the line in Step 1; define sharp where first used.
Pattern risk: answers split evenly (high, in, low, in, low, high), no position pattern. Cues "rumble/air" = low, "squeak/shout" = high are taught, so that is reasoning not guessing.

## L2 Match the note: FAIL
My own words: listen to the note, hear it again in my head, then sing. I can say that, but there is nothing to decide.
1. Step 2: "within half the gap between two neighboring notes on a piano" is meaningless to a non-pianist. Fix: say "close enough that it sounds like the same note" and play an example.
2. Feedback (Try 2 onward) names a result (a shade under, well over) but never says what to do next (go up, go down). Fix: add "sing a little higher/lower" to each result.
3. "You said Under... The app measured it differently. Listen for that next time." says nothing about what decided. Fix: show which way you were off and by roughly how much.
4. No way for a beginner to know if "hearing it in my head" worked; a "hum it silently, then sing" step is only implied. Fix: add an explicit step and a stated order.
5. Check is 5 tries and needs 4. A beginner who is not hearing the note gets no help in the check itself, which is correct, but Step 2 never says what to do after a miss. Fix: a short "if you miss" line.
6. Range exercise is repeated mid-flow (a screen that appears "first"); copy is fine but the "closer than a fifth" error is jargon. Fix: "the two notes were too close together".

## L3 On, under, over, sliding, hunting: FAIL
My own words: put a steady note beside mine; if I move up I was under, down I was over; sliding or wandering shows in how it starts.
Score (answered blind): 6 of 6 practice. Answers are partly by keyword: "wanders" = hunting, "starts low and slides up" = sliding. The feedback does carry reasons.
1. Item 3 (Omar): "lands right on the note a moment late" is ambiguous; it can read as landing on the note, then late. Fix: "starts low and slides up, reaching the note after a moment".
2. Step 7 and the tries ask me to slide from note 1 to note 2 and "the landing is what counts", while Step 4 says to aim at the note so you land at the start. Two opposite instructions. Fix: say plainly that this task is the one place a slide is wanted.
3. Hector (worked example): the point "feeling outranks nothing" is only visible in the last line, hard to see why he checks at a red light. Fix: tell the reader the check beat the reaction before the working.
4. Check is sung slide tries; none of the written questions are in it, so the quiz-like skill (naming the four patterns) is never tested. Fix: add 2 or 3 case questions to the check or drop the unused patterns.
5. "Hear it" buttons list technical names (D4) to the reader. Fix: hide.
6. Flat and sharp are introduced with a bare "people call it"; item 6 uses "flat" before the learner has a rule. Fix: define both at first use.

## L4 Hold it steady: FAIL
My own words: land, keep listening, bring it back. But I cannot say how steady is steady.
1. Step 2 never defines "steady" or how much drift is allowed, nor what the app counts. Fix: state the tolerance in plain words and show a good and a bad hold.
2. No worked example and no case questions: nothing shows what a drift looks like or what to do. Fix: add one example hold with the line drawn.
3. Feedback adds "how long the note stayed steady" but not which way it drifted. Fix: say "drifted down/up after 2 seconds".
4. "Keep the air easy and even: a hold is not a push" appears once; no instruction on what that feels like. Fix: add a short physical cue.
5. Step 7 jumps to a "quarter of the gap" tolerance, which is again unexplained.
6. Check requires 4 of 5 at 2 seconds, but practice moves to 4 seconds; the longest hold taught is not in the check. Fix: align.

## L5 Steps and leaps: FAIL
My own words: hear both notes, notice the gap, aim at the far one. I cannot judge the gap by ear before singing, so "notice how far apart they are" is not a method.
1. "Hear the gap first" gives no way to hear a gap (no steps vs leaps examples to listen to). Fix: add two listen-and-compare buttons.
2. Feedback reports only the last note ("was your last note"), but Step 2 says the usual miss is the second note; the first is never judged. Fix: show both notes.
3. "Small steps come first, then big jumps" is never shown in the screens; a reader cannot tell steps from leaps in the tries. Fix: label each try step or leap.
4. No case questions or worked example. Fix: same as L4.
5. "A quarter of the gap" repeated. Fix: plain wording.

## L6 Short tunes: FAIL
My own words: hear it in my head, sing note by note, hold the last one. No new decision.
1. Step 2 says tunes go from slow to "their speed" but nothing in the tries says or shows speed. Fix: state tempo per group.
2. Result text example says "4 of 5" but the check is 3 tries needing 2. Fix: render the real numbers.
3. "Tries 18 to 18" in the data listing is a visible glitch. Fix: say "Try 18".
4. Feedback for the whole tune gives one result for the last note, so a wrong middle note is never named. Fix: show each note.
5. No guidance on what to do when a note in a longer tune is lost (go back to the start? slow down?). Fix: one line of repair advice.
6. Passing at 2 of 3 is a thin pass for a five-note tune.

## Counts
Verdicts: L1 PASS, L2 to L6 FAIL.
Defects: unclear or undefined terms 9, feedback that does not say what decided or what to do 7, missing method, example or case question 6, internal contradiction or number mismatch 5, wording/answer ambiguity 3 (total 30).
