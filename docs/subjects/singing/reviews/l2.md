## Before

## Notes

- **The why** is short and plain: hear it, then go to it; the line goes away so you rely on your ear. I understood it. "Your own range" is explained only by the later exercise.
- **Step 1, warm-up.** The instruction is clear ("hum a slow slide up and down, nothing to match"). "Hum through a straw" is not explained, but it is only an alternative. Skippable, not scored. It taught me nothing I used later, but it is cheap and I can skip it.
- **Step 2, how a try goes.** Mostly clear. Two problems:
  - "keep going until the line ends". Two paragraphs later "the line" is my voice, drawn on the strip. I could not tell whether "the line ends" means my voice line, the strip, or the singing window. I would guess it means "until the time runs out", but I am guessing.
  - Nothing says how long I need to hold the note or that I should sing it steadily. I am only told to "sing it back on 'oo'".
- **Step 3, find your range** (the first try when no range is stored). This one is good. It tells me exactly what to do ("slide up to the highest note that is easy and hold it", then down). It says to keep it easy. It saves my range and says why. Small gaps: it does not say where to start the slide (low?). There is also a phone microphone permission prompt, which is not mentioned in the lesson. If I tapped "Don't allow", the only message I would get is "I did not hear you. Check that the microphone is on", which would be a reasonable hint.
- **Step 3, tries 2 to 9 (with the line).** One tap on "Hear the note" plays the note (1.5 seconds), then there is a quarter-second gap, then a 1.9-second window. I know what to do: listen, then sing on "oo". I cannot tell from this document whether the screen shows a "sing now" cue when the window opens. A beginner would hesitate at the start of every try without one. The strip shows the note as a bar and my voice as a line: "put the line on the bar". That is a concrete aim, and it is the best teaching idea in the lesson.
- **After a try**, I get one of five phrases: On the note, A shade under, A shade over, Well under, Well over. Plain English. But the screen never tells me what to do about it. "A shade under" means my note was low, so I should aim a bit higher. I would infer that, but a beginner may not. And a miss comes back with a new note, so I cannot try the same note again immediately. The only advice is the "Reason" line ("hear the note in your head before you sing it"), which is one useful tip, repeated after every try.
- **Step 4, no line (tries 10 to 17).** The line disappears suddenly. The strip says "No line this time. Listen, sing, and see how you did afterwards." That is clear. Step 4 is headed "Tries", the same as Step 3, and has no intro of its own saying that the help has been taken away. The "why" paragraph covers it, but only at the start. "8 tries, listed" means nothing to me.
- **Jargon in the try screens:** "Stored: numbers only (how far off each note was, in whole hundredths of a half-step)". If this shows on the screen, it is jargon and a beginner would skip it. If it is only a description for the builder, it should not be in the learner's lesson at all. I cannot tell which.
- **Repeated screens.** Tries 2 to 9, 10 to 17 and 18 to 22 are the same question. That is fine for practice. It is the actual singing, so it is not waste.

## The check

Written as: "5 new tries, with no line. The result, in words, is shown after each try. To pass: At least 4 on the note."

- What is measured: whether I land on the note, without the line, 5 times, on new notes. I understand that.
- How to pass: 4 of 5 "On the note". Clear enough. What counts as "On the note" is never given in numbers on this page (the brief says 25 cents). I do not need the number, but I do not know how close "close" is. I would only learn that from the phrases after each try ("a shade under" etc.).
- Problems:
  - Each check try carries the line "A miss comes back three tries later with a new note." Yet the check says "5 new tries" and tries 18 to 22 only. If a miss comes back, can I keep going until I get 4? If not, why does the line say so? A beginner cannot tell what the pass actually requires. This is copied generic text; it contradicts the 5-try check.
  - Nothing says what happens after "3 of 5: not yet". Do I redo the check? Go back to practice? Which tries come again? A beginner would be left at a dead end at the exact moment they might feel discouraged.
- I cannot pass this check myself (no voice, no ear). It tests single notes in my own range, heard then sung, with no line, scored in the end result's form. That is the form the brief asked for.

## The five answers

- **Gain:** Hear one note and sing it back on pitch, in your own range, without a display. This is a model's judgement from the learner view; no voice was tested.
- **Doing:** Yes. Almost every screen is a sung try (the warm-up, the range slide, and 22 tries). The reading is a few short paragraphs.
- **Check:** Yes on form: new notes in the learner's range, no line, 4 of 5, which is the part's check on new material. I cannot pass it myself. The pass rests on the screens being clear and the check being in the right form; the owner's own try is the test of the voice. The check is blurred by the "a miss comes back" line (above).
- **Clear:** Not fully. Places a beginner would stall:
  1. Step 2 "keep going until the line ends": "line" means two different things.
  2. No sing-now cue is described for the 1.9-second window; the learner view only states the timing.
  3. No advice on what to change after "A shade under / over / Well under / Well over"; only the generic "hear it in your head".
  4. Every check try says a miss comes back, while the check says 5 new tries and 4 on the note: the pass rule is unclear.
  5. Nothing says what to do after "3 of 5: not yet".
  6. The microphone permission prompt is not mentioned; "Stored: ... hundredths of a half-step" is jargon if shown.
  7. Step 4 has no intro (that the line is gone and why), and "8 tries, listed" is meaningless to a learner.
- **Waste:** The warm-up is nearly pure padding (it is skippable and unscored). The repeated "Stored: numbers only..." and "Before the tap..." blocks on every try look like builder notes. The learner view shows them 15+ times. The Reason line repeats the same tip after every try; one tip that never changes teaches little.

## Fixes

1. Step 2: replace "until the line ends" with a plain statement, such as "keep singing until the time runs out", and say how long the window is. Use "line" for one thing only.
2. Show or state a "sing now" cue when the window opens, and put it in the learner view.
3. After each result, add one sentence of what to do next ("A shade under: aim a touch higher"), not only the generic reason. Let the learner hear the same note again before it moves on.
4. Remove "A miss comes back three tries later" from the check tries, or say plainly how a missed check try is handled and what the pass requires.
5. State what happens after "not yet" (retry the check now? which tries repeat?).
6. Add one line about the microphone permission prompt on the first Start.
7. Add a short intro to Step 4 (the line goes away now; why). Remove "listed" and the "hundredths of a half-step" wording from anything the learner sees.
