## L2: Why people dig in, and what is a myth FAIL

Way of deciding, in my words: is the dispute about a checkable fact or a view about a group or policy? A fact gets the correction, and expect a small move. A view gets a true story. If they agreed and then dropped it, count it as a small step. A backfire is a myth.

Defects:
- Accepted-then-dropped items (3, 5, Check 12, 15): "Give the correction again" is also defensible, and Step 2 never rules it out. "Count it as a small step" is an attitude, not an action, so two options can look equally right. The lesson never says what to do next.
- "For a fact, there is a third thing to check" (Step 1): no first or second thing is named. This is confusing.

Notes:
- The move question is fully determined by the first answer, so it adds no new thinking.
- "Hold back / made them hold harder" options are always wrong. Learners can drop them by elimination.
- Step 4 (the theory of reasoning, the skin-cream table) is vague, unnamed, and never tested.
- Words not explained: canvassers, polarized, partisan, nonpartisan.
- Item 4 and Check 17 (a policy topic with a factual dispute inside) briefly made me hesitate between fact and view.
- Item 1 Lou ("Let me see that") is nearly accepted, so "not accepted yet" is close.

## L3: Your goal, and when to leave PASS

Way of deciding, in my words: look at the last few lines. If they answer you, keep going and aim for the small step they offered. If they dig and do not answer, change the subject once, plainly and with no jab. Leave if they threaten or block you, come back after the change, or several people pile on.

Defects (blocking): none.

Guessable:
- "Fair aim" questions: the right option is always the specific, modest step already on the table. The wrong ones are all someone conceding or apologizing.
- "Which line do you say": the right option is the plainest. Item 3's is the shortest. The distractors carry tells such as "once more", "keep quiet" and "step out now".
- "Which line decides" is often the only dig or the only offer.

Unclear:
- Step 2's "however friendly the tone" sits under change-the-subject, yet Check 17 (smiling, door locked) uses it for leave.
- Check 14's distractor "with your basement and all" is nonsense.
- Item 9 and Check 17 have no explicit threat, only a block. Step 2 covers this, but it needed a second read.
