Way of deciding, in my words: ask what each person wants out of the talk right now (to be convinced by reasons, to hit back, to get a deal, to find out, to play to onlookers). Loudness and topic don't matter. Check who the words are for, and recheck every few lines because a talk can change kind.

## Lesson 2: Why people dig in, and what is a myth

Way of deciding, in my words: facts nudge a little and fade, and feelings barely move. Smart people defend their side as well as anyone. You do it too. So "backfire", "dumb" and "facts are pointless" are all myths.

## Lesson 3: Your goal, and when to leave

Way of deciding, in my words: aim small. Keep going if they answer your reasons. Change the subject if it's tipping into a quarrel or show, or they wave away every source, and nobody is hurting you. Leave if they go after you, if a subject change failed, if an audience is using you, or if it isn't safe.
