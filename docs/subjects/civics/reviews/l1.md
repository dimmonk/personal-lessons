## The five answers

- **Gain: yes.** Answering the 17 interview questions of this part from memory, about 10 more than before; named above, each tied to an item.
- **Doing: yes.** One short table, then almost the whole time is answering questions from memory in my own few words, the interview action.
- **Check: yes.** It tests recall of exactly these questions, no options, no help, typed in a few words, in mixed order. For the interview the
  real material is these questions, so the same 17 is right; the novelty is order and no help. Weak point: it comes minutes after practice in
  the same sitting, so it measures short-term recall; the week-apart retention is left to review.
- **Clear: yes, nothing blocks.** Places a beginner stumbles (not blocking, because "My answer means the same" counts in the check):
  1. Q50 refuses "the courts", the table's own word for the judicial branch.
  2. Contractions are not the same as the full words: "doesn't" for "does not" (Q15's printed answer) is a miss. A matcher gap, every subject.
  3. "US" / "U.S." is not accepted where the answer says "(of the) United States" (Q42–45, Q40) or as "U.S. Supreme Court" (Q52).
  4. Fair paraphrases refused: "interprets laws" (Q51), "gives advice to the president" (Q47), "so no branch is / gets too much power" (Q15).
- **Waste:** little. Q42–Q45 are guessable ("the president") but are real interview questions and cannot be cut. The second paragraph of the
  opening prose (the matching rules) could be one sentence; the rest earns its place.

## Fixes (recommended, not blocking)

1. Matcher (`recall.js` `recallTokens`): treat common contractions as their full words ("doesn't" = "does not", "isn't" = "is not",
   "can't" = "cannot") before matching.
2. Matcher or items: accept "US"/"U.S." for "United States" inside the "(of the United States)" bracket, and "(U.S.) Supreme Court" for Q52.
3. Q50: accept "(the) courts", or change the table's "Who" for the judicial branch to "The Supreme Court and federal courts" so the table and
   the accepted answer agree.
4. Add plain equivalents: Q51 "interprets laws"; Q47 "gives advice to the president"; Q15 "so no branch / no one gets too much power" and
   "so no branch is too powerful".
5. Consider `spoken: true` on the check so the questions are heard, as in the interview.
