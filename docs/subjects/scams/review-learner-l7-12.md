# Learner review, scams L7 to L12 (cold beginner, taught L0-L6)

Method: regenerated the views, read each as a newcomer. The views print "(right)" beside answers, so my scores below are my reasoned
answer to each scenario's text, judged before I used the mark as a check. They are not a blind run.

The way of deciding in my own words (L3 rule, carried through L7-L11): ask what it wants; did I start it, or is it exactly what I
agreed; if not, do I deal with them (or is it a government office)? Then check by a channel I already had (app, number on card/bill/quote/
lease, saved number), never the link, number, reply, caller ID, transfer or badge it gives me. If I deal with no one, leave it (and tell
someone I know in person if someone known only online wants money). Looks (spelling, logo, padlock, urgency, kind tone) do not decide.
Pushing (hurry, fear, secrecy, stay on the line) means end the call and check, except on a call I placed myself. L12: sort what went out
(money, password, device, SSN) and do that first step at once.

## Defects that could stall a beginner once (fix before ship if cheap)
1. L9 Item 11 and Check Q20 ("You did buy paint/a TV today", bank text "Was this you?"). Feedback says "You did not start it", but the
   scenario says the learner made the purchase. A beginner reads "I did that, so I started it" and stalls. Fix: say in the scenario or
   reason "you made the purchase, but you did not start this text, and a text is not proof it is your bank".
2. L11 Item 13 (Thistlewick help chat). Slip feedback at turn 1 and 2 ("Install the app") mentions "the $810 you sent", which has not
   appeared yet (it arrives only in turn 3). Fix: give each slip its own outcome per turn.
3. L12 Item 2 second step: "Follow up with the bank in writing" is right but "in writing" is never taught in Steps 1-4 (and "tell them a
   scammer tricked you" does not fit a stolen wallet). Passable by elimination only. Fix: teach it in Step 1 or drop it from the key.
4. L12 Check Q9 (crypto machine): "call the company on its own number" gives no source for the machine operator's number (it is on the
   machine/receipt). Fix: state in the scenario that the operator's number is on the receipt.

## Polish (does not decide the verdict)
5. Every "How do you check?" uses the same stock wrong options (link, reply, search, caller ID callback, "put through to", badge/ID).
   The right option is the odd one out, so a learner could pass by spotting stock wrong answers. Add some items where the wrong options are
   specific to the scenario.
6. In L10 pushing calls, the right reply is always the one that says "hang up and call..." except the deliberate real-call items
   (Item 10, Q28). Good design, but Items 1-7 can be guessed by wording. Vary the wording of the right reply.
7. L10 Item 9: "Tell him to pay in the Bramblecote app instead" is a defensible reply (real sellers say it). It only continues the chat,
   but the end note then says you could have stopped at the first reply. Fix: word it so it is plainly weaker, or accept it as an ending.
8. L9 Items 9 and Q18 "What else do you do?": the option "Tell the friend from the same group, who knows them too" refers to a friend
   not in the scenario. Fix: add the friend to the scenario or reword.
9. L12 second step ("tap both") is mostly common sense (wrong options are "wait", "send more", "call him back") and is not in the pass
   rule. Add one plausible wrong option per item so it teaches.
10. L12 Item 5 distractor feedback "Your own security software does the scan" reads oddly under the option "Pay someone who offers to
    get it back". Reword.
11. L12 Check Q12 (stolen phone, PayLane charge): a new password is also defensible first. The lesson's rule (money first) decides, and
    feedback says so; fine, but mention that the phone is the thing that is in someone else's hands.
12. L8 Check Q24 option "Tell him to send it to the game instead" is unclear (send what, where). Reword.
13. L8/L11: "No government office calls to get your details" is an absolute; "asks for your details on a call you did not place" is safer.
