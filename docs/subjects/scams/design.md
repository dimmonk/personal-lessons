# scams: design record (build-subject skill)

Written 2026-10-10. Gates 2 and 3 are drafts waiting for the owner's approval; until then V69 keeps every lesson of this
subject as it is. The content that exists predates the gates and is audited against this record (gates 4 and 5, designed
blind) before anything is kept.

## Gate 1. Kind of learning and evidence
Kinds: judging, habit. Evidence: `docs/learning-science.md` (to be reorganized by kind of learning); review: `docs/research/review-after-lessons.md`.

## Gate 2. End result (draft)
- After this subject you can: When a message, call or offer asks you to act, you stop, see what it wants, and check it through a channel you already know before doing anything.
- Real moment: A text, email, call or pop-up on your phone or computer, often urgent, often looking real.
- Real-world test: Shown real-looking messages (scams and real ones mixed), you decide for each: act, ignore, or check first, and say how you would check. Real ones are not flagged as scams; scams are never acted on.

## Gate 3. Practice (draft)
Judging many real-looking messages, mixed scams and real, with feedback after each; then a written if-then plan for the moment it happens.
