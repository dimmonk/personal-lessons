# Scams & Social Engineering: plan (2026-10-22)

Research: `docs/research/scams.md` (S1 to S22); general findings `docs/learning-science.md` (P), `docs/research/designing-a-subject.md` (D),
`docs/research/review-after-lessons.md` (R). Replaces the gates of `design.md` (kept as history).

## After this you can

**…pick the right action, and do it, when a text, email, pop-up, chat or call wants something from you (money, a sign-in or
code, your details, or your phone or computer), often urgent, looking real and arriving while you are busy: do it when you
started it, check first through a way you already had when it involves something of yours, leave or end it when it does
not, while handling real messages normally; and if something already went out, make the right first call at once.**

**Real-world test** (the pass mark is ours, set from S12 and P26): 12 new messages in their real form, half genuine, 20 s each,
plus two live calls or chats that push and one "it already happened". Pass: no scam acted on; every check through a way you
already had, never the one the message gave; at most one genuine message mishandled; both exchanges ended without giving a
code, money or access; the first step after a loss right. Taken before (lesson 0, built), at the end, and again unannounced
four weeks later (S19, R4).

## The way of thinking (S1 to S16)

1. **What does it want?** Money, a sign-in or code, your details, your device, or nothing. Not how it looks: polish, logos,
   caller ID, the padlock and knowing your details prove nothing (S1, S3, S9).
2. **Did I start it, just now, here?** (S1, S2)
3. **If it involves something of mine, go in my own way; if not, leave it.** Never the link, number, reply or transfer it
   gives you: those belong to the sender (S2, S4, S5).
4. **Pressure means stop, and a few requests settle it outright**: a code read back, a "safe account", gift cards, crypto or
   a wire, a fee to get money back, money to someone met only online, secrecy. End it; honest senders give you time (S3, S6,
   S11). No channel of your own: tell someone you know in person (S15).
5. **If it already went out, speed decides**: the bank or payment company on its own number first (S13).

## The lessons

Every lesson: the way of thinking first (one screen, P9), one worked case (P10), contrast pairs real against copy (P12, S20), then mixed
timed practice, about half genuine (P26 requires 1), feedback naming what decided and the consequence (R7, S12), then a check of new
cases. Forms are this app's (lesson-standard 3.6, 3.9): the `action` verdict list (`choose`), then a second `choose` "which way" opened
by `when` on "check"; `tap` on the line that asks; `message` blocks (sms, email, popup, chat, call, banner); `pair`; `exchange`; `order`;
timed sets (`seconds: 20`) and `over: 'busy'`; private forms (`own`: setup, plan); retest. Strands one per lesson, mixed in the review.

**Unit 1. The move** (S1 to S5, S12)
- **L1 What does it want?** The five kinds of request and "nothing", on texts, emails, pop-ups and calls. Practice: `tap` the line that asks,
  then `choose` the kind. Check: 10 new, 15 s, 9 right. Why: the request is the experts' trigger (S1); looks are not (S9).
- **L2 Your own way in.** Why anything the contact gives is theirs (the badge number, S5); for each sender, your way (app, card number,
  typed address, order page, family secret word). Practice: `choose` how you would check, options include the message's own link, number
  and "I'll transfer you". Then `own` setup: save the bank's number from the card, the apps, agree the family word (S4, FBI). Check: 8 new,
  8 right (the failure is the message's channel). Why: S4, S5.
- **L3 Act, check or leave.** Built (pilot); unchanged in shape: three questions, worked text, three pairs, eight timed mixed. Check: the
  real-world bar on 12 new. Why: S2, S12, S20.

**Unit 2. Know the scripts** (S7, S10, S11, S16, S19). Each lesson: the scripts side by side with the real thing they copy (`pair`), each
with its tell and its "never" line (S11), the tactic lines marked in feedback (S7); then mixed timed messages with the actions; then one
short `exchange`. Check: 8 new, half genuine, the real-world bar, plus the exchange ended right.
- **L4 Your device**: tech-support pop-up with a number, refund call that wants your screen, a file or link in a message; real install.
- **L5 Your accounts**: copied sign-in page, the code read back, an app that wants too much; real sign-in and code you asked for.
- **L6 Money to strangers**: online friend with a trading app, online partner's emergency, fee to release a prize or loan or job, fee to
  recover lost money; the move with no channel of your own: no money, tell someone in person (S15).
- **L7 Bills and payments**: changed bank details on a real bill, a small fee by link, a fake official demanding gift cards or crypto now,
  the overpaying buyer; the real bill paid the usual way.
- **L8 About you**: identity grab, the friendly stranger's questions, the real request for details you started.

**Unit 3. Under pressure** (S6, S9, S19, S21, S22)
- **L9 What looks prove nothing.** The ten beliefs, each a `prose` block with `tone: 'wrong'`, then a real-looking case where the belief
  would mislead (polished scam, clumsy real bill). Practice and check: the action on 8 such cases, the real-world bar. Why: P29, S9.
- **L10 When it pushes.** The move "end it, call back on your own way", with an exit line to say (S14, S19). Six `exchange`s whose pressure
  rises when the learner holds off (bank "fraud team" with a safe account, grandchild with a cloned voice, boss wanting gift cards,
  courier asking for the code, marketplace buyer's link, online friend's investment), then 10 banners over the busy task (Distraction,
  S6). Check: 3 new exchanges, all ended without code, money or access. Why: S6, S10, S20.
- **L11 Your plan, and all of it.** `own` plan: one if-then cued by a request (examples given); then 12 mixed timed messages from every
  lesson and one exchange, the plan shown before. Check: the real-world test, `retest: 28`. Why: S22, S19, R2 to R4.

**Unit 4. If it already went out** (S13)
- **L12 The first hour.** One screen per loss: money (bank, payment app, wire company, gift card issuer, exchange: at once, ask to reverse),
  a password or code (change it on the real site, then where reused, two-step on), device access (disconnect, scan, change passwords from
  another device), your Social Security number (IdentityTheft.gov, freeze at all three bureaus, free); then report (ReportFraud.ftc.gov),
  keep every message, and the recovery offer that follows is a scam. Practice: 8 situations, `choose` the first step, then `order` the next
  two. Check: 5 new, every first step right. Why: S13; a procedure practised on new cases (R8).

**Returns**: the review brings each strand back at 1, 7 and 24 days, then about every 60 days, with genuine cases in every set (S19, S20, R2).

**Engine gaps**: none needed. Known limits stay as lesson-standard 10.2 has them: calls are transcripts read by an on-device voice;
returns cannot notify without a server (calendar file); the setup is self-reported. The subject record's `parts` (A to F) must be
rewritten to these twelve lessons when the next lesson is built.
