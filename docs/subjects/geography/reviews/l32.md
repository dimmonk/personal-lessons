Reviewer: learner review (Sonnet), 2026-10-19; opened: docs/learner-view/geography-l32.md, .claude/skills/build-subject/review.md

1. Before: given "a town in Mali", I would not know which part of the world to tap.
2. The rule (in my words, from Step 1 before the first case): read the story for the words that name a country, state or coast (the name of the town tells me nothing); say where that is and what it is next to and what water it faces; then tap the part of the world that holds it (the 18-odd parts from earlier lessons, listed in Step 1 with their cut-offs: Mexico is Central America, Russia east of the Urals is Northern Asia). Given before the first case, with the Dubrovnik example.
3. Stuck:
   - The method needs me to know which part a country is in; Step 1 lists the parts but not which country is in which (Uzbekistan, Namibia, Papua New Guinea, Iraq). I had to lean on the earlier parts. The feedback names the part and why, so the lesson teaches it after each miss, but a beginner who skipped those parts would guess.
   - The "traps" (Reno/Los Angeles, Berlin/Toronto, etc.) tell me to compare latitude and longitude, but the questions give no degrees and there is no map, so I cannot work the answer out; I can only answer from my picture of the map (which is the point) and learn from the numbers in the feedback. Reno/San Diego worked example gives its numbers, the items do not. These are practice only and not in the check, so it did not stop me.
   - The three questions in Step 1 (area, touches, water) are not the labels on the worked example (words, where is it, which part); the content is the same.
   - "Russian port on the Pacific" (check): Eastern Europe vs Northern Asia needed the Step 1 sentence about Russia east of the Urals.
4. The check: 10 story places. Traced from the learner view with the Step 1 list and my earlier lessons: I would get Lviv, Basra, Bavaria, Windhoek, Oaxaca, Kingston, Port Moresby, Vladivostok, Lubumbashi, Samarkand if I remembered where those countries are; I am less sure of Papua New Guinea (Melanesia) and the Congo (Central Africa). Not played in the app. Time: about 12 minutes, inside the allowance.
5. After: I can take a story's "deciding words" (the country or coast) and tap the part of the world; I know that two places can lie opposite to what their states or continents suggest (Reno is west of Los Angeles). Tied to the Dubrovnik example, story items 1-10 and the trap feedback.
