Verdict: passes
Reviewer: learner review (Sonnet), 2026-10-19; opened: docs/learner-view/geography-l33.md, .claude/skills/build-subject/review.md

1. Before: I could place some of the world, the US and western Europe; mixed questions from Africa, Asia, Oceania and Latin America would catch me.
2. The rule (in my words, from Step 1 before the first case): no new rule; every question is area first, then what touches the place, then its water, and for a pair of cities, measure it against pairs I know. Each of six kinds (tap a name, name a lit place, flag, neighbour and water, story place, pair of cities) is done once for me before the practice.
3. Stuck:
   - It is a cumulative check of 30 on places from every part. A beginner who skipped a part gets items about places they were never shown; the lesson relies on the earlier parts and says so ("once more").
   - The Belize flag item: tapping Honduras gives a note about the blue-and-white flags of Honduras, El Salvador, Nicaragua and Guatemala. It is true but does not help me tell Belize's flag (blue with a red stripe top and bottom, a coat of arms) from them.
   - The Nevada example says Nevada is "in the middle of the coast"; it is inland, it is just east of California. Mildly confusing but the next sentence fixes it.
   - Nothing used a name before it was shown in this lesson that an earlier part did not teach.
4. The check: 30 questions (12 taps, 4 names, 4 flags, 4 neighbours and water, 3 story places, 3 pairs), pass at 25. Traced from the learner view: the pairs (Los Angeles-Miami, Moscow-Beijing, Cape Town-Sydney) and story places follow from the worked examples; the names, flags and neighbours depend on memory from earlier parts. Not played in the app. Time: reading about 1,100 words plus 8 practice and 30 check items is roughly 18 minutes against the stated 16, within the allowance of one third over.
5. After: I can answer any of the six question kinds from the one method; tied to the six worked examples (Nevada, West Africa, Belgium's flag, what borders Nevada, Dubrovnik, San Francisco-Los Angeles) and the pass of the check.
