## Before
I know the states from earlier lessons but could place almost no big city beyond Los Angeles or Houston by guess.

## The rule
Step 1, before any case: state first, then the part of the state (north/south, coast, shared edge), then the water it stands on. The two worked examples (Houston, Kansas City) show it done in that order.

## Stuck
- Nothing that stops me. Step 1 gives the way to find a city before the first view; the worked cases use only names already shown.
- Minor: the six looking views name rivers and lakes (Columbia, Willamette) not drawn or labelled on every map; the feedback still works.
- Minor: Item 21 treats Minneapolis-St. Paul as one dot, explained in the feedback after the first miss, not before.
- Time: stated about sixteen minutes; my count is 6 min looking, 24 taps at 15 s, 4 names at 30 s, check about 4 min, so roughly 18 to 19. Inside a third over.
- Several items repeat the same long San Francisco / San Jose slip text on wrong taps; heavy but harmless.

## The check
12 questions (8 tap, 4 name) drawn from the 24 cities, no help. Following the lesson, I could answer the tap ones by state then part; the name ones need the dot's state and part (San Francisco, Portland, San Antonio, Omaha are the examples shown). Reachable from the lesson. Score not measured (read, not played).

## After
I can place the 24 cities by state and part, and tell Houston from New Orleans, Kansas City from St. Louis, San Francisco from San Jose, Austin from San Antonio, each tied to its tell-apart line.
