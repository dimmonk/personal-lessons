## Before
I know the French and German flags and little else. Belgium and Germany blur; the Dutch and Luxembourg flags I would mix up.

## The rule
Given before the first case (Step 1): count the stripes and which way they run, say the colors in order from the left or the top, look for a shield or a cross, check the one line that tells it from the look-alike, then find the country on the map with the three questions. In my words: stripes, direction, colors, mark, then the pair line. Passes this test.

- Flags are shown as emoji. They look fine on a phone; I cannot tell the exact shade difference between the deep blue (Netherlands) and the light blue (Luxembourg) beyond the written line, but the lines say which is lighter and the question gives it back. Not blocking on a phone.

- The reason for the order (why stripes first) is not given; only the order.
- Items I could answer without learning: Switzerland (white cross) and France are easy from before. Portugal's flag is unique and answerable by elimination.
