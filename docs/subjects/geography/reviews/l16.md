## Before
I know France and Spain from the earlier lessons and can point at a blank western Europe only roughly. I could not place Belgium, Luxembourg, Liechtenstein or the other tiny countries, and could not say what touches Austria or Switzerland.

## The rule
Given before the first case (Step 1): ask what bigger area it is in, what touches it, what water it is on, then find the area first (Iberia, Low Countries, Alps, the boot). Steps 2-4 then give each area with a named map and a tell-apart line for each look-alike pair. Given.

## Stuck
Nothing that would stop me. Rough spots: the feedback water lists for France and Germany repeat the phrase 'part of the Atlantic Ocean' several times and read as clutter; the five tiny countries are dots that need a zoom (the lesson says to pinch and names stand off on lines); Czechia, Hungary and Slovenia first appear in the Austria feedback without being introduced, but only one neighbour is needed so this does not block. Every screen was used; no item could be answered without the lessons (each of the 15 places has a view, an area and a tell-apart line).

## The check
Pass bar is 8 of 10, using only the rule. The draw is 6 tap, 2 name, 2 border from the 15 places. Every place and every water answer (Landlocked for Switzerland, Austria, Luxembourg, Liechtenstein, the three inland tiny ones; North Sea for the Netherlands and Belgium) can be reached from Steps 2-7. I judge I would get about 9 of 10; the likely misses are the two tiny-country taps. Estimated 16 minutes against the stated 18.
