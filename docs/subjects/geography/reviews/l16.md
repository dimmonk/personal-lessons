## Before
Having done the world and US lessons, I know the Area / Touches / Water habit. I could not reliably place Belgium, Luxembourg, Liechtenstein or the tiny countries, and I mixed up Austria and Switzerland.

## The rule
Given in Step 1, before any case: ask what bigger area it is in, what touches it, what water it is on. Then Iberia, Low Countries, Alps and Italy as the areas. In my words: find the area first, then pin the spot by its neighbors, then say coast or inland.

## Stuck
- Nothing blocking.
- Minor: Austria's feedback lists Czechia, Slovakia, Hungary and Slovenia, which this lesson never shows. A beginner cannot tap them, but any one neighbor is accepted, so it costs nothing. Worth a short note that they are outside this lesson.
- Minor: feedback lines are long (about 60 words each), and it is the main time cost.
- Italy has no "tell apart" line, but nothing in the lesson mixes it up.
- The 11 tap-only places (Portugal, Spain, France, Luxembourg and others) are never asked to be named or bordered in the check. That is by design.
- Every item needed the lesson: the map has no names, so I could not answer by guessing the wording.

## Length
Stated: about 18 minutes. My count:
- Reading, about 900 words at 200 wpm: 4.5 min.
- 3 worked examples, within the reading above.
- 15 taps at 15 s: 3.75 min.
- 4 typed names at 30 s: 2 min.
- 4 neighbor-and-water questions at 20 s: 1.3 min.
- Reading feedback on 23 answers at about 15 s: about 6 min.
- Total: about 17 to 18 min, plus a few re-asked misses.

This is within a third over the stated figure.

## The check
Done without the lesson open, 10 questions drawn from the sample forms: 6 tap, 2 name, 2 border.
- Taps: Portugal, Spain, France, Italy right. Luxembourg and Liechtenstein are the likely misses, as they need the zoom.
- Names: Belgium and Switzerland right.
- Borders: Switzerland, with France and then Landlocked, right. Netherlands, with Belgium and then North Sea, right.
- Score: about 8 to 9 of 10. Passes the pass line of 8.

## After
- Place the 15 countries on a blank map by finding the area first: Step 1 and the taps.
- Say the neighbors and water for Belgium, the Netherlands, Austria and Switzerland: Steps 5 to 7 and the border questions.
- Tell the Austria / Switzerland, Belgium / Netherlands / Luxembourg, Andorra / Monaco and San Marino / Vatican City pairs apart: the Tell apart lines, repeated in the feedback.
- Name a lit country: the name questions.
- Hear "border between Belgium and France" in the news and picture it: the Why and Step 5.
