## Before
I know the regions and the coasts from l2. I cannot reliably place Idaho, Nevada, Utah and the like. I could probably find California, and Alaska and Hawaii.

## The rule
Given in Step 1, before any case: the three questions (Area, Touches, Water). Here the area is the West in two halves, the Pacific side (Washington, Oregon, California, with Alaska and Hawaii in boxes) and the mountain half inland, Montana to New Mexico. Find the half first, then the state. The feedback gives Area, Touches and Water after each answer. In my words: "Which half, then what sits next to it, then the sea or Landlocked."

## Stuck
- Step 2 gives four named screens before any practice. This is a lot to hold, but the tap practice follows straight away and the worked examples show the method.
- Only two pairs get a "Tell apart" line: Wyoming/Colorado and Idaho/Montana. Nevada, Utah, Arizona and New Mexico have only their reasons after an answer. I would probably mix up Nevada and Utah once. The question comes back, so this is not blocking.
- Name questions in the lesson cover only Wyoming, Colorado and Idaho, and the check can draw any of the 13. That is fine, because the names are on screen in Step 2 and tapped many times.
- Border questions list Nebraska, South Dakota and Kansas as neighbors. I have not learned those yet. Because I only need any one neighbor, I tap Utah, Idaho and so on. Nothing blocks me.
- The slip lines (Wyoming/Colorado, Idaho/Montana) show up even when I tap those states while looking for Washington. They are harmless.
- Alaska and Hawaii in boxes are explained in Step 2 and in the reasons. No screen went unused, and no item could be answered without the lesson.

## The check
12 questions: 7 tap, 2 name, 3 border. Need 10. By following the lesson I get the Pacific states, the corner states (Arizona and New Mexico by their notes), Alaska and Hawaii, and Wyoming/Colorado/Idaho/Montana by their pairs. Border is easy: tap any neighbor, then the water or Landlocked. Risk is one slip between Nevada and Utah and one on a name spelling. Estimated 11 of 12, a pass.

## After
- Place all 13 West states on a blank map by finding the half first: Steps 2 to 5.
- Name the four hardest ones, Wyoming, Colorado, Idaho and Montana, by their tell-apart lines: Q1 to Q4.
- Say what water a West state is on, or that it is landlocked, and tap one neighbor: Steps 4 and 6 and the border questions.
- Name a lit West state: Q14 to Q16.

## Time (against "about fifteen minutes")
- Reading: Steps 1 to 4, the "look for about a minute" screens and the 20 reasons come to about 2,000 words, so about 10 minutes at 200 wpm. Fewer reasons are actually read, so a lower real figure is likely.
- Taps: 13 at 15 s is 3.3 minutes.
- Typed names: 3 at 30 s is 1.5 minutes.
- Neighbor-and-water: 4 at 20 s is 1.3 minutes.
- Check: 7 taps, 2 names and 3 borders, about 4 minutes.
- Total about 15 to 17 minutes with a few repeats. That is well inside a third over, so within budget.
