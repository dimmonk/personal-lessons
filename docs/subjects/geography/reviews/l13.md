## Before
I know Mexico is south of the US. Central America is a blur of small countries.

## The rule
Step 1, before the first case: find the area (Mexico; the northern isthmus Guatemala, Belize, El Salvador, Honduras; the southern isthmus Nicaragua, Costa Rica, Panama), then what touches it, then its water. The step also says plainly that the world map counts Mexico in Central America but here Central America means the seven countries below Mexico, so I was not misled.

## Stuck
- Nothing blocking. "Isthmus" is explained as a thin land bridge.
- Belize, El Salvador and the Panama Canal are tiny on a phone; zoom and the named views make it doable.
- Gulf of Mexico against Caribbean Sea is the likeliest confusion; the tell-apart line and slip cover it.
- Time: three views, 19 practice items, 4 questions, a 12-question check; stated 14 minutes, about 13 to 15 by my count.

## The check
Sample draw: Mexico tap right; El Salvador name right; El Salvador border (Guatemala, then Pacific) right. Pass bar 10 of 12 is tight but fair after the repeats.

## After
I can place all eight countries, the Panama Canal and both seas, say Guatemala has coasts on both seas, El Salvador only the Pacific, Belize only the Caribbean, and name any one neighbour plus water for Honduras, El Salvador, Nicaragua and Costa Rica. Taught by Steps 2 to 7.
