# Learner review, round 3: Strategy l1 to l14

Role: cold learner (project manager, two small businesses, no formal training). Read only docs/learner-view/strategy-l1.md to l14.md. Nothing was run. I answered every practice and check question from the text before reading the marked answer.

Verdicts: l1 FAIL. l2 to l14 PASS. Blocking defects: 2 (both l1). Minor, non-blocking notes: 6.

## Way of deciding, in my words (before the first practice question)

- l1: find the line that says what is in the way; no such line = goal/wish (no steps) or to-do list (steps); challenge + one approach + steps that serve it = strategy.
- l2: find the line showing whether rivals do the same work better (run it better) or whether I am already level and squeezed/copied anyway (different position).
- l3: value = most customer would pay minus least staff/suppliers would take; split three ways; a move grows value only if it moves one of the two ends.
- l4: find the line showing who takes the money (buyers, suppliers, newcomers, substitutes, rivals); compare sizes; growth goes to whoever is not blocked.
- l5: earnings = brought in minus direct cost; core = distinctive line that earns most; losing line: fix, keep and trim, or let go.
- l6: does the new work pull against the old on the same people/hours/room? Dropped one side = trade-off, kept both = straddle, no pull = fits.
- l7: hold each activity to the position; the misfit serves another customer or the trade's usual way.
- l8: does the difference give a higher price or lower cost, and what stops a copy (only-ours, fit, trust/records, nothing).
- l9: same customer and same activities = deepens; other activities or other customer = blurs; money does not decide.
- l10: who will you serve and why will they pick you, and do the two fit; name the trap otherwise.
- l11: list what must be true, test the one least likely (fact against before talk before evidence).
- l12: start from the record of many alike; predict rival's answer; check the reason for "it worked there" holds here.
- l13: can anyone see buyers? record if yes, small trial if no; stake inside what I can lose.
- l14: run the six in order.

## l1 Goal, wish, or strategy? FAIL (2 defects)

1. Item 5 (Ray, plumbing), tap question. Two lines say what is in the way: "We lose about one job in four because we cannot reach emergency calls the same day" and the step "Raise prices 5%, because the parts we buy cost more this year and we have not raised our prices since 2022". Step 1 itself says a step can carry a "because" and the test is whether the line says what is in the way; the price line does (rising costs). Both defensible, key accepts only the first. Fix: drop the because-clause from the price step (e.g. "Raise prices 5%.") or make the reason a non-obstacle.
2. Check question 14 (Mike, car wash), tap question. Same flaw: "Buy new brushes, because the old ones leave streaks on dark cars and customers have started to complain" names a problem (complaints) as clearly as "Rainy weeks bring no cars, but full wages". Key accepts only the rainy-weeks line. Fix: strip the because-clause from the brushes step, or the learner who reasons from the lesson text loses a pass-rule point unfairly (the check forbids misreading goal vs strategy, but a missed tap here also costs the 5-of-6).

Everything else in l1 was clear on one read.
