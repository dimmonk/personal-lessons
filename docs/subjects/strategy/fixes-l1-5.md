# Fixes to Strategy lessons 1 to 5 after the two reviews (2026-10-23)

Reviews: `review-learner-l1-5.md` (L = learner, numbered per lesson) and `review-research-l1-5.md` (R = research, numbered 1 to 21).
Each finding, what was done, and where the root was. "Disputed" means kept as is, with the reason.

## Lesson 1

- **R1 (a running-better plan counts as a strategy).** Root: the rule had no limit. Step 1 now says a challenge that is only doing the same
  work worse than rivals is a plan to run the business better, not a strategy, and that telling the two apart comes next. Lesson 2 gained a
  look-alike pair of two plans with a strategy's shape (a running gap; a new position). Research F gained the reconciliation of Rumelt and
  Porter as a judgement call.
- **R2 (lesson order unsourced).** Marked as a judgement call with its reason in plan.md ("Judgement calls ...") and the lesson's header.
- **R3 (overclaim).** "One of the field's best-known teachers tests a plan ...".
- **L1 (step vs wish).** Step 1 defines a step (something you can put on a calendar or buy) and a wish (a quality you hope for), with
  "a great experience for every member" named as a wish.
- **L2 (incomplete kernels).** Step 1 says all three parts must be there; a challenge and an approach with no steps is a strategy not
  finished. Not practised as an item: the verdict list has no fourth answer, and Rumelt's kernel calls the actions its substance.
- **L3 (mission, vision).** Defined in Step 1 as statements of what a company stands for, so wishes.
- **L4 (pattern).** Approach-like lines with no challenge ("So we push harder ...", "This year we focus on families ...", "So we put the
  children first ...") put into a to-do list, a to-do list and a goal; two strategies now have a six- and an eight-word challenge; approach
  lines no longer all start "So we".

- **L6 (press vs copied machines).** The reason now says a newer press only brings the shop level, and level is all it needs: the trouble

- **R4 (tap not in the plan; one deciding line).** Partly disputed: the tap stays, since finding the fact that shows the cause is the skill

  says the fact that explains the loss decides.
- **R5 (gym rent workable).** Rent is now "as low as any gym's in town" and the chains' buying for hundreds of gyms is out of reach.

## Lesson 3

- **R6 (a move's own cost left out).** Root: the rule. A move now grows the value when it raises what customers would pay by more than it
  adds to the work, or lowers what suppliers and staff would take; it shrinks the value when it adds more than customers would pay for it.
  New verdict `shrinks` and slip `added-cost-missed` (subject.js lists). Every "grows" move states its added cost (or that it adds none);
  three "shrinks" moves added (p-gold as a look-alike beside p-photo, q-polish, k-bandana). The check is 3 sales and 4 moves, 6 of 7.
- **R7 (price rise and priced-out customers).** "As long as every customer still buys, ...".
- **R8 (overclaim).** "Harvard Business School's strategy course is built on this picture."
- **R9, L4 (tap shape; the verdict read off the dollar line).** The tap is dropped from the moves: the verdict now weighs two numbers, and
  "shrinks" moves name dollars too, so a dollar line no longer gives the answer away. p-week now grows the value with no dollar named.
- **R10 (estimate dropped).** Recorded in plan.md.
- **L1 (cost vs the least they would take).** Step 1 adds the cleaner paid $20 who would have worked for $17.
- **L2 (pay cut "less happy").** Removed.
- **L3 (top/bottom of a bar drawn left to right).** Feedback now says "the customers' end" and "the suppliers' end", which hold for any drawing.
- **L5 (forced cut taught once).** No change; the reviewer judged it acceptable, and p-squeeze sits beside p-week in the first group.
- **L6 (same three number asks).** Disputed: the three numbers are the stick's three shares, the procedure the plan wants practised to
  fluency; the generator varies which share is biggest, so the order of sizes cannot be learned.

## Lesson 5

- **R18 (core = best-paying only).** Root: the rule. The core is now the line that is distinctive (customers come for it rather than to a
  rival) and earns the most; a line every rival sells the same way is not the core however much it earns. Every table's notes state what is
  distinctive; three items (p-lube, q-moves, k-lunches) have a plain line that earns more than the distinctive core.
- **R19, L3 (the share's denominator).** Sourced and taught one way: the core's share of what all the lines earn together, losses taken off
  (new ST19, Kaplan and Narayanan's whale curve, via secondary sources), which can pass 100%. The slip `loser-counted` is removed; the share
  over the earning lines only is now the named slip `loss-left-out`, with its reason, since the taught share is sourced. The learner's own work now uses the same share and names the single top line,
  the lesson's idea, instead of "N lines make more than half".
- **R20 (keep-or-drop rule unsourced; staff time).** Marked as our judgement call in plan.md. A direct cost is now "one that would stop if
  the line stopped" in Step 1 and in the own form's help.
- **R21, L1, L2 (kinds mixed; overlapping lines).** Every table is one kind, named in its title ("product by product", "customer by
  customer", "way of selling by way of selling"); "Restaurant menus" is now "Six restaurants"; Priya's table is four ways of selling with
  each order counted once.
- **R11 (no pair).** Two item pairs in the first group: the biggest seller beside the core on two surfaces (p-crews, p-printers), and a
  distinctive top earner beside a core that earns less than a plain line (p-tutors, p-lube).
- **L4 (own work skips the tie question).** Each losing line in the own work now asks whether the core's customers buy it with the core.
- **L5 ("Keep it and grow it" never right).** Root: an option no case could make right. The losing-line ask now offers fix or let go; the
  verdict `keep` is removed from the list.
- **L6 (lines earning evenly never practised).** Root: the own work printed a case the lesson never taught. That message is removed; the own
  work names the top line and asks the distinctiveness question.
- **L7 (core at its limit).** "If it cannot grow, protect it."
- **L8 (overheads).** One sentence: rent is paid from what the lines earn together, so a business can earn on most lines and still lose.
- **L9 ("marked after the answer").** Disputed: that marker is written by the learner-view renderer
  (tools/learner-view/render-learner-view.mjs) to tell a reader which rows the app marks once the question is answered; the lesson
  itself shows no such words, so nothing in the lesson changes.
