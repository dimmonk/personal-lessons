## Defect

1. **Lesson 10, check question 15 (Ali), `items-choice.js:190`.** Plan 2, "Do what the big car-wash chains do ... Run all of it
   more smoothly than they do", is marked copycat only. It also fits the lesson's head-on trap ("going first at the strongest
   rival, on the ground where it is strongest"): it names the big chains and competes with them on their own offer. A learner
   who picks head-on is marked wrong on a check question with a 5-of-6 pass rule. The source distinguishes the two
   (program-of-the-month is "generic industry strategies"; Don Quixote is head-to-head with the strongest). **Fix:** make the
   plan generic, not aimed at named strong rivals: "Do what every car wash in the county does: a monthly pass, an app and a
   free vacuum."
