# Geography: Europe, west

Revision 2, draft (the screen carries the draft line). Part: Europe, west. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

The news says a strike has shut the border between Belgium and France. If you cannot picture where Belgium is, what touches it and what water it is on, the story tells you little. In twelve minutes you will place the fifteen countries of western Europe on a blank map, name the ten larger ones and tell them by their flags, and say what touches each of those.

## Step 1: A worked example

Belgium


Above the map: **Tap it on the map.**
The map of Europe, west with Belgium lit and the tap shown on it.
The working, one step at a time:
1. Find it from a country you know. Find France first, then Belgium just north of it, on the North Sea.

Then the answer: Belgium. Belgium is a small country on the North Sea between France and the Netherlands, and its capital, Brussels, is where the European Union has many of its offices.
