# Geography: Türkiye, the Levant, Iraq and Iran

Revision 1, live. Part: Türkiye, the Levant, Iraq and Iran. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A story says a ship crossed the Persian Gulf, or that a road between Jordan and Syria has reopened. If this part of the map is a blur, you cannot tell which country is which, or who is next to whom. In about fifteen minutes you will place Türkiye, the five countries of the Levant, Iraq, Iran, the Bosporus and the Persian Gulf on a blank map, by finding the area each sits in first, then name the four most often mixed up and say what touches each.

## Step 1: How to find a country

The same three questions as for any place. What bigger area is it in? That tells you where to look. What touches it? Its neighbors pin the spot, and they are what the news names. What water is it on? A coast, a short coast or none at all is often the point of the story. This part of the world is the Middle East. On the world map Türkiye, the Levant and Iraq are Western Asia, but Iran is in South Asia; they are taken together here because they are neighbors that the news calls the Middle East. It has three areas. Türkiye is the big peninsula across the top, where Europe meets Asia. The Levant is the lands along the east end of the Mediterranean: Syria, Lebanon, Israel, Palestine and Jordan. Iraq and Iran are the two big countries east of them. Find the area first, then the country. After every answer the feedback gives you the three: Area, Touches, Water. It takes about fifteen minutes: four views, three questions done for you, then the ten places found, four named and four with what touches them.

## Step 2: Türkiye and the Bosporus

Look for about half a minute at each of four views. Say each name as you find it. None of it stays up: next you find places on a map with no names. Türkiye is the big country across the top of the region, a peninsula between the Black Sea in the north and the Mediterranean in the south, with the Aegean Sea on its west coast. The Bosporus is the narrow strait at its northwest corner, where Europe meets Asia.

## Step 3: The Levant

The Levant is the lands along the east end of the Mediterranean. Syria is the big country in the north. Down the coast, from north to south, come Lebanon and Israel, with Palestine in two pieces beside Israel: the West Bank inland and Gaza on the coast. Jordan lies inland, below Syria and east of Israel and Palestine.
