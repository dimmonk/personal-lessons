# Geography: Central Asia and the Caucasus

Revision 1, live. Part: Central Asia and the Caucasus. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A story names Kazakhstan's oil, a border clash between Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan, or talks between Armenia and Azerbaijan. If the -stans are one blur to you, you cannot picture who is next to whom. In about fourteen minutes you will place the eight countries of Central Asia and the Caucasus, and the Caspian Sea, on a blank map, by finding the area each sits in first, then name the four most often mixed up and say what touches each.

## Step 1: How to find a country

The same three questions as for any place. What bigger area is it in? That tells you where on the map to look. What touches it? Its neighbors pin the exact spot, and they are what the news names. What water is it on? Coast or no coast is often the point of the story. This region has two areas. Central Asia, the part of Asia from part 1c, holds five countries whose names end in -stan: Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan. The Caucasus, a mountain land to the west, holds three: Georgia, Armenia and Azerbaijan. The Caspian Sea lies between the two areas. The world map of part 1c groups them differently: Kazakhstan and its four neighbors are Central Asia there, but Georgia, Armenia and Azerbaijan are Western Asia, with Türkiye. Here the three of the Caucasus stand beside Central Asia because the two lie either side of the Caspian Sea, and the feedback says what the world map calls each. Find the area first, then the country. After every answer the feedback gives you the three: Area, Touches, Water. It takes about fourteen minutes: three views, three questions done for you, then the nine places found, four named and four with what touches them.

## Step 2: The five -stans

Look for about a minute at each of three views. Say each name as you find it. None of it stays up: next you find places on a map with no names. Five countries in the middle of Asia have names that end in -stan, which means land. Kazakhstan is the huge one across the top. Uzbekistan is the one in the middle. Turkmenistan is the southwest one, on the Caspian Sea. Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan are in the southeast. Afghanistan and Iran lie to their south.
