# Geography: Europe, the Balkans and the south

Revision 1, live. Part: Europe, the Balkans and the south. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

The news says a ferry has sunk in the Aegean, or that a border crossing between Serbia and Kosovo has closed. If the Balkans are one gray blur to you, you cannot picture where, who is next door, or what water is near. In about fifteen minutes you will place the twelve countries from Slovenia to Malta, with the Adriatic and the Aegean, on a blank map, by finding the area each sits in first, then name the four most often mixed up and say what touches them.

## Step 1: How to find a country

The same three questions as for any place. What bigger area is it in? That tells you where on the map to look. What touches it? Its neighbors pin the exact spot, and they are what the news names. What water is it on? Coast or inland is often the point of the story. On the world map these countries are not one region: Slovenia to Greece and Malta are Southern Europe, Bulgaria is Eastern Europe and Cyprus is in Western Asia. Here they are taken together, the Balkans and the islands near them, so they can be found together. The areas are four. The north-west of the Balkans is Slovenia, Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina and Serbia, behind the Adriatic. The south-west and middle is Montenegro and Albania on the coast, with small Kosovo and North Macedonia inland, and Greece at the bottom. The east is Bulgaria, on the Black Sea. The islands are Malta and Cyprus, far out in the Mediterranean. Find the area first, then the country. After every answer the feedback gives you the three: Area, Touches, Water.

## Step 2: The north-west: Slovenia, Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia

Look for about 40 seconds at each of four views. Say each name as you find it. None of it stays up: next you find places on a map with no names. The Balkans are the land south of Austria and Hungary, between the Adriatic on the west and the Black Sea on the east. Slovenia is the small country at the head of the Adriatic, with Italy and Austria, from the map of western Europe, beside it. Croatia curls round Bosnia and Herzegovina along the Adriatic coast. Serbia is the big inland country behind them.
