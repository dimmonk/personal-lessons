# Geography: The parts of the Americas, Europe and Africa

Revision 1, live. Part: The parts of the Americas, Europe and Africa. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A story says a ship sank off West Africa, or that a storm hit Central America. Those are parts of a continent, and every country you meet later sits in one. In about twelve minutes you will tap the twelve parts of North America, Europe and Africa, each found from its continent first, and name the ones most often mixed up.

## Step 1: How to find a part of a continent

The same three questions. Area: which continent is it in? Find the continent first. Touches: which side of the continent is it on, and what parts are beside it? Water: which ocean or sea is it on? The feedback after each answer gives the three. Next, one map per continent, with its parts lit and named; none of them stays up.

## Step 5: Two names that look alike

North America is the whole continent. Northern America is its top part only: Canada, the United States and Greenland. Central America and the Caribbean are its other two parts.

## Step 6: A worked example

What is the lit part called?

A map of The world, north up, with no names on it. Lit: West Africa. The learner can pinch to zoom, drag to move it and use the buttons to zoom in, zoom out and show the whole map.

The working, one step at a time:
1. Area: which bigger area is it in? The lit part is inside Africa, so it is one of Africa's five parts.
1. Touches: what is next to it? It is the bulge on the left, with Northern Africa above it and Central Africa to its right, as the map of Africa's five parts showed.
1. Water: what water is it on? It is on the Atlantic, the west side, so it is West Africa.

Then the answer: West Africa. West Africa is the bulge of Africa on the Atlantic, south of Northern Africa and west of Central Africa.
