# Geography: The flags of East Asia and mainland Southeast Asia

Revision 1, live. Part: The flags of East Asia and mainland Southeast Asia. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A flag in a news photo tells you whose it is only if you can read it. In about nine minutes you will read the eleven flags of East Asia and mainland Southeast Asia, and tell the look-alikes: China, Vietnam and Taiwan; Laos, Cambodia and North Korea; Japan and South Korea.

## Step 1: How to read a flag

Read it the same way every time. First the ground: one color, or bands, and do the bands lie flat or stand upright? Then the colors in order, from the top or the left. Then the mark: a star, a disc or a temple. Then the one line that tells it from the flag it is mistaken for: that is where the misses are. Last, find the country: its area, what touches it, its water. Next, the eleven flags in four groups; none of it stays up.

## Step 2: Red flags

Look for about a minute at each of four groups. Say each name as you read its flag.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇨🇳 China; 🇻🇳 Vietnam; 🇹🇼 Taiwan.

China: Red with a large yellow star and four small ones in the top left corner. Vietnam has one big star in the middle.

Vietnam: Red with one large yellow star in the middle. China has four small stars as well, in the corner.

Taiwan: Red with a blue rectangle in the top left corner that holds a white sun. China and Vietnam have yellow stars and no blue.

## Step 3: Flags of flat red and blue bands

These three are flat red and blue bands.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇱🇦 Laos; 🇰🇭 Cambodia; 🇰🇵 North Korea.

Laos: Three flat bands, red, a wider blue and red, with a plain white disc. Cambodia and North Korea have blue on the top and bottom.

Cambodia: Three flat bands, blue, a wider red and blue, with a white temple on the red. Laos has red on the top and bottom.

North Korea: Three flat bands, blue, a much wider red and blue, with a thin white line on each side of the red and a red star in a white disc on the left of the red. Cambodia has a white temple instead.

## Step 4: White flags

These two are white.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇯🇵 Japan; 🇰🇷 South Korea.

Japan: White with a large red disc. South Korea has a red and blue swirl.

South Korea: White with a red and blue swirl and a black mark of bars in each corner. Japan has a plain red disc.

## Step 5: Flags of other stripes

Only Mongolia's stripes stand upright.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇹🇭 Thailand; 🇲🇲 Myanmar; 🇲🇳 Mongolia.

Thailand: Five flat stripes, red, white, a wider blue, white and red.

Myanmar: Three flat stripes, yellow, green and red, with a large white star in the middle.

Mongolia: Three upright stripes, red, blue and red, with a yellow symbol on the left. The only flag here with upright stripes.

The map of East Asia with Laos lit and the tap shown on it.
The working, one step at a time:
1. Read the flag: how many stripes, which way they run, which colors. Three bands, lying flat, red on top, a wider blue in the middle and red at the bottom, with a white disc in the blue.
1. Tell it from the flag it is mistaken for. Cambodia and North Korea have the same two colors with blue on the top and bottom. Red on the top and bottom, and a plain white disc, make this one Laos.
1. Find the country. Laos is the landlocked country in the middle of mainland Southeast Asia, with Vietnam on its east and Thailand on its west. Tap it.

Then the answer: Laos. Three flat bands, red, a wider blue and red, with a plain white disc. Cambodia and North Korea have blue on the top and bottom.
