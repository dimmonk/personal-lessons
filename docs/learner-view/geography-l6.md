# Geography: The South, the Atlantic side

Revision 1, live. Part: The South, the Atlantic side. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A friend flies into Charlotte, or the news is from Washington, D.C. If the Atlantic side of the South runs together for you, you cannot place either. In about fifteen minutes you will place its eight states and D.C., by going down the coast, name the ones most often mixed up and say what touches four of them.

## Step 1: How to find a state

The same three questions as for any place. What bigger area is it in? What touches it? What water is it on? Here the area is the South's Atlantic side: a line of states down the Atlantic coast, from Delaware and Maryland to Florida, with West Virginia inland behind Virginia and Washington, D.C. between Maryland and Virginia. Find the coast first, then count down it. After every answer the feedback gives you the three: Area, Touches, Water.
