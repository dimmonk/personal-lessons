# Geography: The flags of Southern Africa

Revision 1, live. Part: The flags of Southern Africa. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A flag in a news photo, on a truck or at a match tells you whose it is only if you can read it. In about thirteen minutes you will read the flags of the twelve countries of Southern Africa, by the stripes, the colors and the mark, and tell the ones that look alike: Zimbabwe and Mozambique, South Africa and Namibia, Botswana, Lesotho and Eswatini, Madagascar and Mauritius.

## Step 1: How to read a flag

Read it the same way every time. First see whether it has stripes and which way they run, flat, upright or slanting: that alone splits these flags in groups. Then say the colors in order, from the top or from the left, since look-alikes often share colors in a different order. Then look for a mark, a triangle, a star, a shield or a bird, which settles a flag the stripes do not. Then check the one line that tells it from the flag it is mistaken for: that is where the misses happen. Last, find the country on the map, using the three questions: the area it is in, what touches it, the water it is on. Next, the twelve flags in four groups, each with its line; none of it stays up.

## Step 2: Flat bands with a mark in the middle

Look for about half a minute at each of four groups. Say each name as you read its flag. The bands here lie flat across the flag, with a mark in the middle.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇦🇴 Angola; 🇲🇼 Malawi; 🇱🇸 Lesotho; 🇸🇿 Eswatini.

Angola: Two flat bands, red over black, with a gold star and half a cogwheel crossed by a machete in the middle. Mozambique has a gold star and a tool too, but on green, black and yellow stripes.

Malawi: Three flat stripes, black, red and green, with a red rising sun in the black stripe.

Lesotho: Three flat bands, blue, white and green, with a black hat in the white. Eswatini has red in the middle and a shield.

Eswatini: Blue, a wide red band edged in yellow, and blue, with a large black and white shield, two spears and a staff across the red. Lesotho has white and green and a black hat.

## Step 3: A triangle at the left

These three have a triangle on the left.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇿🇼 Zimbabwe; 🇲🇿 Mozambique; 🇿🇦 South Africa.

Zimbabwe: Seven flat stripes, green, yellow, red, black, red, yellow and green, with a white triangle on the left holding a red star and a gold bird. Mozambique has three stripes and a red triangle.

Mozambique: Three flat stripes, green, black and yellow, with thin white lines between them, and a red triangle on the left holding a yellow star with a book, a rifle and a hoe. Zimbabwe has seven stripes and a white triangle.

South Africa: Red over blue, split by a green band that forks into a Y lying on its side at the left, round a black triangle edged in yellow. Namibia has one slanting red band.

## Step 4: A slant, or an upright band

In these three the lines do not lie flat: Namibia slants, Zambia has three upright bands under an eagle, and Madagascar has an upright band at the left.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇳🇦 Namibia; 🇿🇲 Zambia; 🇲🇬 Madagascar.

Namibia: A wide red band slanting up from the lower left corner to the upper right, edged in white, with a blue triangle holding a yellow sun above it and a green triangle below it. South Africa has a green Y on its left side.

Zambia: Green, with an orange eagle flying over three upright stripes, red, black and orange, at the right end.

Madagascar: A white upright band on the left, then red over green on the right. Mauritius has four flat stripes.

## Step 5: Plain flat stripes

These two have flat stripes and no mark at all.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇧🇼 Botswana; 🇲🇺 Mauritius.

Botswana: Light blue with a black stripe across the middle, edged in white, and nothing else on it.

Mauritius: Four flat stripes, red, blue, yellow and green from the top. Madagascar has a white band on the left.
