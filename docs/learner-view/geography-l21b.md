# Geography: The flags of Arabia and North Africa

Revision 1, live. Part: The flags of Arabia and North Africa. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A flag in a news photo, on a ship or at a match tells you whose it is only if you can read it. In about twelve minutes you will read the flags of the thirteen countries of Arabia and North Africa, by the stripes, the colors and the mark, and tell the ones that look alike: Egypt, Yemen and Sudan; the Emirates and Kuwait; Qatar and Bahrain; Tunisia, Algeria and Morocco.

## Step 1: How to read a flag

Read every flag the same way. First count the stripes and see which way they run: that splits most of these flags in two. Then say the colors in order, since look-alikes share colors and differ in one. Then look for a mark, a bird, a star or a saw-edge, because the mark settles two flags with the same stripes. Then check the one line that tells it from the flag it is mistaken for: that is where the misses happen. Last, find the country by the three questions: its area, what touches it, its water. Next, the thirteen flags in five groups; none of it stays up.

## Step 2: Red, white and black stripes

Look for about half a minute at each of five groups. Say each name as you read its flag. These three have the same flat stripes, red, white and black from the top; what is on the stripes tells them apart.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇾🇪 Yemen; 🇪🇬 Egypt; 🇸🇩 Sudan.

Yemen: Three flat stripes, red, white and black from the top, with nothing on them. Egypt has an eagle; Sudan a green triangle.

Egypt: Three flat stripes, red, white and black from the top, with a gold eagle on the white one. Yemen has no eagle; Sudan has a green triangle.

Sudan: Three flat stripes, red, white and black from the top, with a green triangle on the left. Yemen has no mark; Egypt has an eagle.

## Step 3: Flat stripes and a mark on the left

These three have flat stripes and something on the left side.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates; 🇰🇼 Kuwait; 🇴🇲 Oman.

United Arab Emirates: Three flat stripes, green, white and black from the top, with a wide red bar on the left. Kuwait has red at the bottom.

Kuwait: Three flat stripes, green, white and red from the top, with a black trapezoid on the left. The Emirates have black at the bottom.

Oman: Three flat stripes, white, red and green from the top, with a wide red bar on the left that holds a white dagger and crossed swords.

## Step 4: A white saw-edge

These two are a shade of red with a white saw-edge down the left. Count the points.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇶🇦 Qatar; 🇧🇭 Bahrain.

Qatar: Dark maroon, with a white saw-edge on the left that has nine points. Bahrain is lighter red, with five.

Bahrain: Red, with a white saw-edge on the left that has five points. Qatar is darker, with nine.

## Step 5: A star in the middle

These three each have a star, and two have a crescent too.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇹🇳 Tunisia; 🇩🇿 Algeria; 🇲🇦 Morocco.

Tunisia: Red, with a white disc in the middle that holds a red crescent and star. Morocco has a green star and no disc.

Algeria: Two upright halves, green and white, with a red crescent and star in the middle. Tunisia is all red.

Morocco: Plain red, with a green five-pointed star in the middle and no disc. Tunisia has a white disc, a crescent and a star.

## Step 6: The other two

Each of these is built a different way.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇱🇾 Libya; 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia.

Libya: Three flat stripes, red, black (twice as wide) and green, with a white crescent and star on the black.

Saudi Arabia: Plain green, with white Arabic writing and a white sword under it.
