# Geography: The flags of Mexico and Central America

Revision 1, live. Part: The flags of Mexico and Central America. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A flag in a news photo, on a truck or at a match tells you whose it is only if you can read it. In about ten minutes you will read the flags of Mexico and the seven countries of Central America, by the stripes and what is on them, and tell the look-alikes: Honduras, El Salvador and Nicaragua (flat stripes), and Guatemala (upright).

## Step 1: How to read a flag

Read every flag the same way. First count the stripes and see which way they run, upright or flat. Then say the colors in order. Then look at what sits on the middle stripe, a star or an emblem. Then check the line that tells it from the flag it is mistaken for: that is where the misses happen. Last, find the country with the three questions: its area, what touches it, its water. Next, the eight flags in three groups; none of it stays up.

## Step 2: Flags of upright stripes

Look for about a minute at each of three groups. Say each name as you read its flag. These stripes stand upright.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇲🇽 Mexico; 🇬🇹 Guatemala.

Mexico: Three upright stripes, green, white and red, with an eagle holding a snake, perched on a cactus, on the white one.

Guatemala: Three upright stripes, light blue, white and light blue, with a coat of arms (a quetzal, a scroll, crossed rifles and swords, in a wreath) on the white one.

## Step 3: Flags of blue, white and blue

All three have flat stripes, blue, white and blue. What is on the white stripe tells them apart.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇭🇳 Honduras; 🇸🇻 El Salvador; 🇳🇮 Nicaragua.

Honduras: Three flat stripes, blue, white and blue, with five blue stars arranged in an X on the white one. El Salvador and Nicaragua have an emblem instead.

El Salvador: Three flat stripes, blue, white and blue, with a round emblem on the white one, ringed all the way round by words. Nicaragua's emblem is a triangle with its words on arcs above and below; Honduras has stars.

Nicaragua: Three flat stripes, blue, white and blue, with a triangle emblem on the white one, its words on an arc above it and an arc below it. El Salvador's emblem is round, ringed all the way round by words; Honduras has stars.

## Step 4: The other flags

Each of these is built a different way.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇨🇷 Costa Rica; 🇵🇦 Panama; 🇧🇿 Belize.

Costa Rica: Five flat stripes, blue, white, a wide red one, white and blue, with a white oval holding the coat of arms toward the left of the red one.

Panama: Four squares: white with a blue star at the top left, red at the top right, blue at the bottom left, white with a red star at the bottom right.

Belize: Royal blue, with a thin red stripe at the top and the bottom and a white disk in the middle holding a coat of arms ringed by a green wreath.
