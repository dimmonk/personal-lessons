# Geography: Arabia and North Africa

Revision 1, live. Part: Arabia and North Africa. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A story says a ship has run aground in the Suez Canal, oil tankers are waiting at the Strait of Hormuz, or a border has closed between Algeria and Morocco. If Arabia and North Africa are a blur to you, you cannot picture who is next to whom or which water is meant. In about sixteen minutes you will place the thirteen countries, the Red Sea, the Suez Canal and the Strait of Hormuz on a blank map, by finding the area each sits in first, then name three look-alikes and say what touches four countries.

## Step 1: How to find a country

The same three questions as for any place. What bigger area is it in? That tells you where on the map to look. What touches it? Its neighbors pin the exact spot, and a border is what the news is about. What water is it on? Coast or inland, and which sea, often is the story. On the world map Arabia is Western Asia and Egypt, Sudan, Libya, Tunisia, Algeria and Morocco are Northern Africa; they are taken together here because the Red Sea and the Suez Canal join them. There are four areas. The Arabian Peninsula holds the three big countries: Saudi Arabia, Yemen and Oman. The Gulf coast holds Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar and the United Arab Emirates. The Nile countries, Egypt and Sudan, are the north-east corner of Africa. The Maghreb, the north-west of Africa, holds Libya, Tunisia, Algeria and Morocco. Three waters link them: the Red Sea, the Suez Canal and the Strait of Hormuz. After every answer the feedback gives you the three: Area, Touches, Water. It takes about sixteen minutes: four zoomed views, three questions done for you, then sixteen places found, three named and four with what touches them.
