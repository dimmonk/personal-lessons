# Geography: The lower 48, mixed

Revision 1, live. Part: The lower 48, mixed. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

In real life a state is never named next to its neighbors: a story jumps from Ohio to Arizona to Georgia. In about ten minutes you will place states from all four regions in one mixed run, each found from its region first, until the whole country holds together.

## Step 1: All four regions at once

Now the states come mixed, from every region. For each one, the same three questions: which region is it in, the West, the Midwest, the South or the Northeast? Which part of that region, and what touches it? What water is it on? Find the region first, then the state.

## Step 2: Questions

21 questions, shuffle, with each inner pair asked one after the other, in a random order, with about 20% from earlier lessons mixed in, unlabeled. A missed question comes back at least three questions later, until it is right. After each answer: the right answer, the deciding words, the reason, and one line on the learner's own choice if it was wrong.
