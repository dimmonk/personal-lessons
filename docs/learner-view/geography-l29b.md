# Geography: The flags of Island Southeast Asia and the Pacific

Revision 1, live. Part: The flags of Island Southeast Asia and the Pacific. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A flag in a news photo tells you whose it is only if you can read it. In about fourteen minutes you will read the flags of the twenty countries of island Southeast Asia and the Pacific, by how each is built and by its colors, and tell the pairs that look alike: Australia and New Zealand, Fiji and Tuvalu, Indonesia and Singapore, Samoa and Tonga, Palau and the Federated States of Micronesia.

## Step 1: How to read a flag

Read it the same way every time. First see how it is built: a Union Jack in the corner, flat stripes, a diagonal or a triangle, or one mark on a plain field. That sorts these twenty into four groups to start with; a few flags fit two, and each flag has its own line. Then say the colors in order, from the top or the left, because look-alikes share colors in a different order. Then find the mark, a star or a bird, which settles what the layout does not. Then check the line that tells it from the flag it is mistaken for: that is where the misses happen. Last, find the country: its area, what is beside it, its water. Next, the twenty flags; none of it stays up.

## Step 2: Flags with the Union Jack

Look for about a minute at each of four groups. Say each name as you read it. These four carry the Union Jack in the upper left corner.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇦🇺 Australia; 🇳🇿 New Zealand; 🇫🇯 Fiji; 🇹🇻 Tuvalu.

Australia: Dark blue, with the Union Jack in the upper left, a large white star under it and five white stars on the right. New Zealand has four red stars and no large star.

New Zealand: Dark blue, with the Union Jack in the upper left and four red stars edged in white on the right. Australia has white stars and a large star under the Union Jack.

Fiji: Light blue, with the Union Jack in the upper left and a shield on the right. Tuvalu's light blue has nine yellow stars there.

Tuvalu: Light blue, with the Union Jack in the upper left and nine yellow stars on the right. Fiji has a shield there.

## Step 3: Flags of flat stripes

The stripes here lie flat, in two bands or more. The Philippines and Vanuatu also have a triangle at the left, and Malaysia a blue square in the corner.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇮🇩 Indonesia; 🇸🇬 Singapore; 🇲🇾 Malaysia; 🇵🇭 Philippines; 🇻🇺 Vanuatu; 🇰🇮 Kiribati.

Indonesia: Two flat bands, red over white, with nothing on them. Singapore has a crescent and stars on the red.

Singapore: Two flat bands, red over white, with a white crescent and five white stars in the upper left. Indonesia has the same bands with nothing on them.

Malaysia: Fourteen flat stripes, red and white in turn, with a blue square in the upper left holding a yellow crescent and a yellow star. Indonesia and Singapore have only two bands.

Philippines: Two flat bands, blue over red, with a white triangle at the left holding a yellow sun and three yellow stars.

Vanuatu: Two flat bands, red over green, with a black triangle at the left inside a yellow sideways Y, holding a yellow boar's tusk and two crossed fern leaves.

Kiribati: Red above, with a yellow bird flying over a yellow rising sun; blue below, with three wavy white stripes.

## Step 4: Flags cut on the diagonal, or with a triangle

These five have a diagonal line or a triangle.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇵🇬 Papua New Guinea; 🇸🇧 Solomon Islands; 🇧🇳 Brunei; 🇹🇱 Timor-Leste; 🇲🇭 Marshall Islands.

Papua New Guinea: Cut on the diagonal from the upper left: the upper right is red with a yellow bird of paradise, the lower left is black with five white stars.

Solomon Islands: A thin yellow diagonal from the lower left to the upper right: a blue triangle above it with five white stars, a green triangle below.

Brunei: A yellow flag crossed by two diagonal bands, white above black, with a red emblem in the middle.

Timor-Leste: A red flag with a yellow triangle at the left and, on it, a black triangle holding a white star.

Marshall Islands: Blue, with two stripes widening from the lower left, orange above white, and a white star with long rays in the upper left.

## Step 5: Flags with one mark on a plain field

The last five have no stripes: one mark on a plain color.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇼🇸 Samoa; 🇹🇴 Tonga; 🇵🇼 Palau; 🇫🇲 Federated States of Micronesia; 🇳🇷 Nauru.

Samoa: Red, with a blue square in the upper left holding five white stars. Tonga has a white square with a red cross.

Tonga: Red, with a white square in the upper left holding a red cross. Samoa has a blue square with white stars.

Palau: Light blue, with a yellow disk a little to the left of the middle. The Federated States of Micronesia has four white stars.

Federated States of Micronesia: Light blue, with four white stars in a diamond in the middle. Palau has a yellow disk.

Nauru: Dark blue, with a thin gold stripe across the middle and a white star under it at the left.
