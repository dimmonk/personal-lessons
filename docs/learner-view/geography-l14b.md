# Geography: The flags of the Caribbean

Revision 1, live. Part: The flags of the Caribbean. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A flag on a ship, a stadium or a news photo tells you whose it is only if you can read it. In about fifteen minutes you will read the flags of the thirteen countries of the Caribbean, by how each is built and by its colors, and tell the pairs that look alike: Haiti and the Dominican Republic, Barbados and Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Trinidad and Tobago and Saint Kitts and Nevis, the Bahamas and Saint Lucia.

## Step 1: How to read a flag

Read it the same way every time. First see how it is built: flat stripes, upright stripes, a diagonal band, a cross, or one shape on a plain field. You see that at a glance, and it sorts these thirteen into five groups. Then say the colors in order, because look-alikes share colors in a different order. Then look for the mark, a triangle, a trident, stars or a coat of arms, which settles a flag the layout does not. Then check the line that tells it from the flag it is mistaken for: that is where the misses happen. Last, find the country on the map by the three questions: the group it is in, what is beside it, the water it is on. Next, the thirteen flags in five groups, each with its line; none of it stays up.

## Step 2: Flags of flat stripes

Look for about a minute at each of five groups. Say each name as you read its flag. The stripes here lie flat, with a mark at the left or, for Haiti, in the middle.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇨🇺 Cuba; 🇭🇹 Haiti; 🇧🇸 Bahamas.

Cuba: Five flat stripes, blue and white in turn, with a red triangle on the left holding a white star.

Haiti: Two flat bands, blue over red, with a white panel in the middle holding the coat of arms (a palm tree). The Dominican Republic has a white cross.

Bahamas: Three flat stripes, aquamarine, gold and aquamarine, with a black triangle on the left. Saint Lucia is light blue with no stripes.

## Step 3: Flags of upright stripes

The stripes here stand upright, side by side.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇧🇧 Barbados; 🇻🇨 Saint Vincent and the Grenadines.

Barbados: Three upright bands, blue, gold and blue, with a black trident head on the gold. Saint Vincent and the Grenadines ends in green, with diamonds.

Saint Vincent and the Grenadines: Three upright bands, blue, a wide gold and green, with three green diamonds in a V on the gold. Barbados is blue, gold, blue.

## Step 4: Flags with a diagonal band

These two have one wide band cut on the diagonal, from corner to corner.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇹🇹 Trinidad and Tobago; 🇰🇳 Saint Kitts and Nevis.

Trinidad and Tobago: A red flag with a black diagonal band edged in white, from the top left to the bottom right. Saint Kitts and Nevis has its band the other way, between green and red.

Saint Kitts and Nevis: A black diagonal band edged in yellow, with two white stars, runs from the bottom left to the top right: green above it, red below. Trinidad and Tobago has its band the other way, on red.

## Step 5: Flags with a cross

A cross runs across these flags to the edges. Jamaica's is slanted; the other two stand upright.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇯🇲 Jamaica; 🇩🇴 Dominican Republic; 🇩🇲 Dominica.

Jamaica: A gold diagonal cross splits the flag into four triangles: green above and below, black at the left and right.

Dominican Republic: A white cross reaches the edges and divides the flag into four blocks, blue and red, with a small coat of arms in the middle. Haiti's is two flat bands.

Dominica: A green flag with a cross of three bands, yellow, black and white, and a red disk in the middle holding a parrot ringed by ten green stars.

## Step 6: Flags with a shape on a plain field

The last three have no stripes: one shape or border on a field of one color.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇱🇨 Saint Lucia; 🇬🇩 Grenada; 🇦🇬 Antigua and Barbuda.

Saint Lucia: A light blue flag with a black triangle edged in white, above a smaller yellow triangle, in the middle. The Bahamas has stripes.

Grenada: A red border with six yellow stars around a field split into yellow and green triangles, with a red disk and a yellow star in the middle and a nutmeg on the left.

Antigua and Barbuda: A red flag with a triangle pointing down from the top edge, in three bands: black with a yellow rising sun, then blue, then white.
