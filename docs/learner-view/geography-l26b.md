# Geography: The flags of South Asia

Revision 1, live. Part: The flags of South Asia. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A flag in a news photo or at a match tells you whose it is only if you can read it. In about nine minutes you will read the eight flags of South Asia and tell the ones that look alike.

## Step 1: How to read a flag

Read it the same way every time. Count the stripes and see which way they run: upright, flat or across a corner. Say the colors in order, from the left or the top. Look for a mark: a crescent, a wheel, an animal. Check the line that tells it from the flag it is mistaken for: that is where the misses happen. Last, find the country by its area, what touches it and its water. Next, the eight flags in three groups; none of it stays up.

## Step 2: Flags with green and a round mark

Look for about a minute at each of three groups. Say each name as you read its flag. Pakistan and Bangladesh are green fields; the Maldives is red with a green rectangle in the middle.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇵🇰 Pakistan; 🇧🇩 Bangladesh; 🇲🇻 Maldives.

Pakistan: Dark green with an upright white band on the left, and a white crescent and star on the green. The Maldives' crescent is on a green rectangle in red.

Bangladesh: Dark green with a red disc a little left of the middle. The Maldives is red with a green rectangle.

Maldives: Red with a green rectangle in the middle holding a white upright crescent. Pakistan's crescent is on plain green, with a star.

## Step 3: Flags of bands

India's three bands lie flat; Afghanistan's stand upright. Since 2021 the Taliban government has flown a plain white flag with black writing; the three-band flag is the one before, and the one the World Factbook still describes.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇮🇳 India; 🇦🇫 Afghanistan.

India: Three flat bands, orange (saffron), white and green, with a blue wheel in the white. Afghanistan's three bands stand upright.

Afghanistan: Three upright bands, black, red and green, with a white emblem in the red. India's three bands lie flat.

## Step 4: Flags with an animal, or a shape of their own

Sri Lanka has a lion and Bhutan a dragon.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka; 🇧🇹 Bhutan; 🇳🇵 Nepal.

Sri Lanka: Dark red with a gold lion holding a sword, a gold border, and a narrow panel on the left with a green band and an orange band. Bhutan's animal is a dragon.

Bhutan: Cut along the diagonal, yellow above on the left and orange below on the right, with a white dragon along the line. Sri Lanka's animal is a lion.

Nepal: The only flag that is not a rectangle: two red triangles, one above the other, with a blue edge, a white moon in the top one and a white sun in the bottom one.
