# Geography: The parts of Asia and Oceania

Revision 1, live. Part: The parts of Asia and Oceania. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A story says prices rose across Southeast Asia, or that talks are under way in the Middle East. Those are parts of a continent, and every country you meet later sits in one. In about ten minutes you will tap the six parts of Asia and the two of Oceania, each found from its continent first, and name the ones most often mixed up.

## Step 1: How to find a part of a continent

The same three questions. Area: which continent is it in? Find the continent first. Touches: which side of the continent is it on, and what parts are beside it? Water: which ocean or sea is it on? The feedback after each answer gives the three. Next, one map per continent, with its parts lit and named; none of them stays up.

## Step 4: A worked example

What is the lit part called?

A map of The world, north up, with no names on it. Lit: Southeast Asia. The learner can pinch to zoom, drag to move it and use the buttons to zoom in, zoom out and show the whole map.

The working, one step at a time:
1. Area: which bigger area is it in? The lit part is inside Asia, the biggest continent, so it is one of Asia's six parts.
1. Touches: what is next to it? It is at the bottom right of Asia, below East Asia and east of South Asia, as the map of Asia's parts showed, with its many islands.
1. Water: what water is it on? It sits between the Indian Ocean and the Pacific, so it is Southeast Asia.

Then the answer: Southeast Asia. Southeast Asia is the part of Asia south of China, from Myanmar and Vietnam down to the islands of Indonesia and the Philippines.
