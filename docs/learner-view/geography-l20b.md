# Geography: The flags of Türkiye, the Levant, Iraq and Iran

Revision 1, live. Part: The flags of Türkiye, the Levant, Iraq and Iran. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A flag in a news photo, on a truck or at a match tells you whose it is only if you can read it. In about twelve minutes you will read the flags of Türkiye, the Levant, Iraq and Iran, by the stripes, the colors and the mark, and tell the ones that look alike: Jordan, Palestine and Syria, and Iran and Iraq.

## Step 1: How to read a flag

Read it the same way every time. Count the stripes and see which way they run. Say the colors in order from the top, since look-alikes often share colors in a different order. Look for a mark, a triangle, a star, a tree or writing, which settles a flag the stripes do not. Check the one line that tells it from the flag it is mistaken for: that is where the misses happen. Last, find the country on the map, using the three questions: the area it is in, what touches it, the water it is on. Next, the eight flags in three groups; none of it stays up.

## Step 2: Flat stripes in black, white and green

Look for about half a minute at each of three groups. Say each name as you read its flag. These three have flat stripes in the same three colors.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇯🇴 Jordan; 🇵🇸 Palestine; a flag whose emoji may show an older design, drawn here: three flat stripes, green, white and black from the top, with three red stars in a row on the white Syria.

Jordan: Three flat stripes, black, white and green from the top, with a red triangle on the left that holds a white star. Palestine has the same, with no star.

Palestine: Three flat stripes, black, white and green from the top, with a red triangle on the left. Jordan has the same, with a white star in the triangle.

Syria: Three flat stripes, green, white and black from the top, with three red stars on the white. Jordan and Palestine have the same colors the other way up, with a red triangle on the left.

## Step 3: Flat stripes with writing

These two have three flat stripes with white in the middle, and a mark made of Arabic writing.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇮🇷 Iran; 🇮🇶 Iraq.

Iran: Three flat stripes, green, white and red from the top, with a red emblem in the middle of the white. Iraq has red on top and black below, with green writing on the white.

Iraq: Three flat stripes, red, white and black from the top, with green writing on the white. Iran has green on top and red below, with a red emblem on the white.

## Step 4: A mark on red or white

These three each have one mark that no other flag here has.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇹🇷 Türkiye; 🇱🇧 Lebanon; 🇮🇱 Israel.

Türkiye: A plain red field with a white crescent and a white star. No other flag here is red with a crescent.

Lebanon: Red, white and red flat stripes, the white one twice as wide, with a green cedar tree in the middle.

Israel: White, with a blue six-pointed star in the middle and a blue stripe along the top and the bottom.
