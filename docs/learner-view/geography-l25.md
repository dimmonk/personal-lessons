# Geography: Southern Africa

Revision 1, live. Part: Southern Africa. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A story says a cyclone has hit Mozambique and Malawi, or a drought has reached Zambia and Zimbabwe, or workers are crossing from Lesotho into South Africa. If Southern Africa is one blur to you, you cannot picture who is next to whom, or who has a coast. In about fifteen minutes you will place the twelve countries of Southern Africa, with the Kalahari, on a blank map, by finding the area each sits in first, then name the four most often mixed up and say what touches each.

## Step 1: How to find a country

The same three questions as for any place. What bigger area is it in? That tells you where on the map to look. What touches it? Its neighbors pin the spot, and they are what the news names. What water is it on? A coast, an island or the inland is often the point of the story; here water means a sea, an ocean or one of the big lakes (Victoria, Tanganyika, Malawi), so a river or a small lake does not count. Southern Africa has five areas. Along the Atlantic, in the west, are Angola and Namibia. Along the Indian Ocean, in the east, is Mozambique. Between the two coasts, with no sea of their own, are the inland states: Zambia, Zimbabwe, Malawi and Botswana, with the Kalahari, a desert, mostly in Botswana. At the southern tip is South Africa, with Lesotho inside it and Eswatini beside it. Out in the Indian Ocean are two island countries, Madagascar and Mauritius. Here the whole south of the continent is taken as one. On the world map of part 1b, Southern Africa is only its bottom five: Namibia, Botswana, South Africa, Lesotho and Eswatini. Mozambique, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Malawi and Madagascar are East Africa there, and Angola is Central Africa; the tiny island of Mauritius is not on that map at all. The feedback says which. Find the area first, then the country. After every answer the feedback gives you the three: Area, Touches, Water. It takes about fifteen minutes: four views, three questions done for you, then the thirteen places found, four named and four with what touches them.

## Step 2: The two coasts

Look for about half a minute at each of four views. Say each name as you find it. None of it stays up: next you find places on a map with no names. Angola and Namibia are on the Atlantic, along the west coast: Angola in the north, Namibia below it. Mozambique is the long country on the Indian Ocean, along the east coast. Everything between the two coasts is inland.
