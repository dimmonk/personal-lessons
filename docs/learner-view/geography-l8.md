# Geography: The cities west of the Mississippi

Revision 1, live. Part: The cities west of the Mississippi. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A storm is hitting Houston, or a story is datelined Kansas City, and you cannot say which state it is in or where in the state. In about sixteen minutes you will place the 24 big cities west of the Mississippi on a blank map, by finding the state first and then the part of it and the water, then name the four most often mixed up from the lit dot.

## Step 1: How to find a city

A city is found the way any place is, from the bigger area down. The state comes first: you have placed every state, so it cuts the map down to one shape you know, and a miss then lands inside the right state. Then the part of the state: north or south, the coast, the edge it shares with another state. Last, the water it stands on, the ocean, a lake or a river, since most of these cities grew on water. West of the Mississippi means by state: Minnesota, Iowa, Missouri, Arkansas, Louisiana and every state beyond them, in six views. This east and west split is made only for these cities: the world map you met first puts all of them in one part, Northern America. After every answer the feedback gives you the state and the part of it, the water, and what tells the city from the one it is mixed up with.

## Step 2: California

Look for about a minute at each of six views. Say each name as you find it. None of it stays up: next you find the cities on a map with no names. California holds five. Los Angeles and San Diego are in the south; San Francisco and San Jose share a bay in the north; Sacramento is inland.

## Step 8: A worked example

Houston


Above the map: **Tap the city on the map.**
The map of The United States with Houston lit and the tap shown on it.
The working, one step at a time:
1. Area: which state is it in? Houston is in Texas, the big state along the Gulf, which you placed in The South, from Texas to Kentucky.
1. Touches: where in the state, and next to what? In Texas it is in the southeast, the part nearest Louisiana, not up north with Dallas–Fort Worth.
1. Water: what water is it on? The Gulf of Mexico, the sea along the bottom of the country, is a short way to its southeast. Tap the southeast of Texas, near the Gulf.

Then the answer: Houston. Tell it from New Orleans: Houston is in Texas; New Orleans is east of it, in Louisiana.

What is the lit city called?

Then the answer: Kansas City. Kansas City is mostly in Missouri, not in Kansas; the smaller Kansas City, Kansas, is just across the state line.
