# Geography: The flags of Europe, central and east

Revision 1, live. Part: The flags of Europe, central and east. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A flag in a news photo, on a truck or at a match tells you whose it is only if you can read it. In about ten minutes you will read the flags of the nine countries from Poland to Russia, by the stripes and the colors, and tell the pairs that look alike: Poland and Czechia, Russia and Slovakia, Romania and Moldova.

## Step 1: How to read a flag

Read it the same way every time. First count the stripes and see which way they run, upright or flat: that alone splits most flags into groups. Then say the colors in order, since look-alikes often share their colors. Then look for a mark, a triangle, a shield or an eagle, which settles a flag the stripes do not. Then check the one line that tells it from the flag it is mistaken for: that is where the misses happen. Last, find the country on the map by the area it is in, what touches it and its water. Next, the nine flags in three groups, each with its line; none of it stays up.

## Step 2: Flags of two flat stripes

Look for about a minute at each of three groups. Say each name as you read its flag. These four have two stripes, one over the other.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇵🇱 Poland; 🇨🇿 Czechia; 🇺🇦 Ukraine; 🇧🇾 Belarus.

Poland: Two flat stripes, white on top and red below. Czechia has the same two stripes with a blue triangle on the left.

Czechia: Two flat stripes, white on top and red below, with a blue triangle on the left pointing into them. Poland has the same two stripes and no triangle.

Ukraine: Two flat stripes, blue on top and yellow below.

Belarus: Two flat stripes, red on top and twice as tall as the green below, with a white band down the left side that holds a red pattern.

## Step 3: Flags of three flat stripes

These three have three stripes lying flat.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇭🇺 Hungary; 🇷🇺 Russia; 🇸🇰 Slovakia.

Hungary: Three flat stripes, red, white and green, with the red on top.

Russia: Three flat stripes, white, blue and red, with nothing on them. Slovakia has the same three stripes and a shield on the left.

Slovakia: Three flat stripes, white, blue and red, with a red shield on the left that holds a white double cross. Russia has the same three stripes and no shield.

## Step 4: Flags of three upright stripes

These two have three stripes standing upright, in the same colors.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇷🇴 Romania; 🇲🇩 Moldova.

Romania: Three upright stripes, blue, yellow and red, with the blue on the left and nothing in the yellow. Moldova's has an eagle there.

Moldova: Three upright stripes, blue, yellow and red, like Romania's, with an eagle on a shield in the middle of the yellow.
