# Geography: The flags of West Africa, the coast

Revision 1, live. Part: The flags of West Africa, the coast. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A flag in a news photo, on a ship or at a match tells you whose it is only if you can read it. In about thirteen minutes you will read the twelve flags of the West African coast and tell apart the six that share the green, yellow and red of the pan-African colors.

## Step 1: How to read a flag

Read every flag the same way. First say the colors. Green, yellow and red together are the pan-African family, six flags on this coast, so the order and the marks are what tell them apart. Then see which way the stripes run, upright or flat, and whether a band stands down the left. Then look for a mark, a star, a square or a ring. Then check the one line that tells it from the flag it is mistaken for. Last, find the country by the three questions: its area, what touches it, its water. Next, the twelve flags in four groups; none of it stays up.

## Step 2: Green, yellow and red in three stripes

Look for about a minute at each of four groups. Say each name as you read its flag. These three have green, yellow and red in three stripes.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇸🇳 Senegal; 🇬🇳 Guinea; 🇬🇭 Ghana.

Senegal: Three upright stripes, green, yellow and red, with the green on the left and a small green star in the yellow. Guinea has the same stripes the other way round, red on the left, and no star.

Guinea: Three upright stripes, red, yellow and green, with the red on the left and no mark. Senegal has them the other way round, green on the left, with a green star.

Ghana: Three flat stripes, red, yellow and green, with a large black star in the yellow. Senegal and Guinea have upright stripes.

## Step 3: Green, yellow and red with a band or a square

These three have the same colors laid out differently.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇬🇼 Guinea-Bissau; 🇧🇯 Benin; 🇹🇬 Togo.

Guinea-Bissau: A red upright band on the left with a black star, and two flat stripes beside it, yellow on top and green below. Benin has a green band on the left, with yellow over red.

Benin: A green upright band on the left, and two flat stripes beside it, yellow on top and red below. Guinea-Bissau has a red band with a black star on the left, and yellow over green.

Togo: Five flat stripes, green and yellow in turn, with a red square at the top left holding a white star. Ghana has only three stripes and no square; no other flag on this coast has five stripes.

## Step 4: Upright stripes with white in the middle

Both have three upright stripes with white between, and no yellow or red.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇳🇬 Nigeria; 🇨🇮 Côte d'Ivoire.

Nigeria: Three upright stripes, green, white and green, with nothing on them. Côte d'Ivoire's are orange, white and green.

Côte d'Ivoire: Three upright stripes, orange, white and green, with the orange on the left. Nigeria's are green, white and green.

## Step 5: Blue, and red and white

The last four are each their own, and each has blue.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇬🇲 The Gambia; 🇸🇱 Sierra Leone; 🇱🇷 Liberia; 🇨🇻 Cape Verde.

The Gambia: Three flat stripes, red, blue and green from the top, with the blue stripe edged by thin white lines.

Sierra Leone: Three flat stripes, light green, white and light blue, with nothing on them. The Gambia's three flat stripes have red on top, blue between thin white lines, and green below.

Liberia: Eleven flat stripes, red and white in turn, with a blue square at the top left holding one white star. Togo's star is in a red square on green and yellow stripes.

Cape Verde: Blue, with a ring of ten yellow stars on the left and a flat white, red and white band across the lower part.
