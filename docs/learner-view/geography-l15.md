# Geography: South America

Revision 1, live. Part: South America. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A story says a flood has cut off a town in Peru, or fires are burning in the Amazon. If South America is a blank to you, the story tells you little. In about fifteen minutes you will place the twelve countries of South America, French Guiana, the Andes and the Amazon on a blank map, by finding the area each sits in first, then name the five most often mixed up and say what touches four of them.

## Step 1: How to find a place

The same three questions as for any place. What bigger area is it in? That tells you where on the map to look. What touches it? Its neighbors pin the exact spot, and the news names them. What water is it on? Coast or inland often matters. South America cuts easily into areas. The Andes run down the whole Pacific side, with Colombia, Ecuador, Peru and Chile along them and Bolivia inland behind. On the north coast are Venezuela and, east of it, the three small Guianas. Brazil is the giant filling the east and the middle, with the Amazon across its north. The south holds Argentina, Uruguay on the Atlantic and Paraguay inland. Only Bolivia and Paraguay have no coast. After every answer the feedback gives you the three: Area, Touches, Water.

## Step 9: A worked example

What borders Bolivia?


Above the map: **Tap a country next to it, then the water it touches. If no water touches it, tap Landlocked.**
The map of South America with A place next to Bolivia: Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Paraguay and Peru. Landlocked. lit.
The working, one step at a time:
1. Area: which bigger area is it in? Bolivia is inland, high in the Andes, behind Peru and Chile, on the west of the middle of the continent.
1. Touches: what is next to it? Peru is along its north-west side: tap Peru.
1. Water: what water is it on? No sea or ocean reaches it: Peru and Chile are between it and the Pacific, and Brazil is between it and the Atlantic. So it is landlocked: tap Landlocked.

Then the answer: A place next to Bolivia: Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Paraguay and Peru. Landlocked. Tell it from Paraguay: Bolivia is the bigger of the two countries with no coast, in the west, high in the Andes; Paraguay is the smaller one south-east of it.
