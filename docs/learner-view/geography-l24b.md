# Geography: The flags of East Africa and the Horn

Revision 1, live. Part: The flags of East Africa and the Horn. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A flag in a news photo or at a match tells you whose it is only if you can read it. In about nine minutes you will read the flags of the twelve countries of East Africa and the Horn, and tell the pairs that look alike: Kenya and South Sudan, Somalia and Djibouti.

## Step 1: How to read a flag

Read it the same way every time. First the stripes: which way do they run, flat or slanting? Then the colors in order, from the top or the left, since look-alikes share colors in a different order. Then a mark: a triangle, a disc, a shield or a star. Then the one line that tells it from the flag it is mistaken for. Last, find the country by its area, what touches it and its water. Next, the twelve flags in three groups, each with its line; none of it stays up.

## Step 2: Flags of flat stripes

Look for about half a minute at each of three groups. Say each name as you read its flag. These have flat stripes with a mark on them.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇪🇹 Ethiopia; 🇺🇬 Uganda; 🇷🇼 Rwanda; 🇰🇪 Kenya.

Ethiopia: Flat stripes of green, yellow and red, with a blue disc holding a yellow star.

Uganda: Six flat stripes, black, yellow and red twice over, with a white disc holding a gray crane.

Rwanda: Flat bands of wide sky blue, yellow and green, with a golden sun on the blue at the right.

Kenya: Flat black, red and green stripes with thin white lines, and a red and black shield with crossed spears. South Sudan has a blue triangle instead.

## Step 3: Flags with a triangle at the left

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇸🇸 South Sudan; 🇩🇯 Djibouti; 🇪🇷 Eritrea; 🇰🇲 Comoros.

South Sudan: Flat black, red and green stripes with thin white lines, and a blue triangle on the left holding a gold star. Kenya has a shield instead.

Djibouti: Light blue over green, with a white triangle on the left holding a red star. Somalia is plain light blue with a white star.

Eritrea: A red triangle on the left, with green above and blue below, and a gold olive wreath in the triangle.

Comoros: Four flat stripes, yellow, white, red and blue, with a green triangle on the left holding a white crescent and four stars.

## Step 4: Flags that slant, or have no stripes

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇸🇴 Somalia; 🇹🇿 Tanzania; 🇧🇮 Burundi; 🇸🇨 Seychelles.

Somalia: Plain light blue with one white star. Djibouti has green under its blue and a white triangle.

Tanzania: A black diagonal band edged in yellow, with green above on the left and blue below on the right.

Burundi: A white diagonal cross with red above and below, green at the sides, and a white disc holding three red stars.

Seychelles: Five bands fanning out from the bottom left corner: blue, yellow, red, white and green.
