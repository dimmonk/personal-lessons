# Geography: The flags of Central Asia and the Caucasus

Revision 1, live. Part: The flags of Central Asia and the Caucasus. Where the subject ends up: After this subject you can put a named place on a map with no names, say what it borders and what water it is on, name a place that is lit, tell a country by its flag, and say roughly how far it is from you, when a place is named in the news, by a person or in a travel plan with no map in sight.

## The why

A flag in a news photo or at a match tells you whose it is only if you can read it. In about ten minutes you will read the eight flags of Central Asia and the Caucasus and tell the three pairs that look alike apart.

## Step 1: How to read a flag

Read every flag the same way. First see whether it is stripes or one plain field with a mark: that splits these eight in two. Then say the colors in order, from the top or the left, since look-alikes often share colors in a different order. Then find the mark, a crescent, a sun, a crown or a cross. Then check the one line that tells it from the flag it is mistaken for: that is where the misses happen. Last, find the country by the three questions: its area, what touches it, its water. Next, the eight flags in three groups; none of it stays up.

## Step 2: Flags of flat stripes

Look for about a minute at each of three groups. Say each name as you read its flag. These stripes lie flat.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇦🇲 Armenia; 🇦🇿 Azerbaijan; 🇺🇿 Uzbekistan; 🇹🇯 Tajikistan.

Armenia: Three flat stripes, red, blue and orange, with no mark on them. Tajikistan's top stripe is red too, but it has a white one and a crown.

Azerbaijan: Three flat stripes, blue, red and green, with a white crescent and one eight-pointed star in the middle of the red. Uzbekistan has a white stripe and its crescent is in the top left.

Uzbekistan: Three flat stripes, blue, white and green, with thin red lines between them and a white crescent and twelve stars in the top left. Azerbaijan's stripes are blue, red and green, with one star in the middle.

Tajikistan: Three flat stripes, red, a wider white one and green, with a gold crown and seven stars in the middle of the white. Armenia's top stripe is red too, but the next two are blue and orange and there is no mark.

## Step 3: Flags with a sun

These two have a gold sun on a plain field.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇰🇿 Kazakhstan; 🇰🇬 Kyrgyzstan.

Kazakhstan: Sky blue, with a gold sun above a soaring gold eagle in the middle and a gold pattern down the left edge. Kyrgyzstan has a sun too, but on red and with no eagle.

Kyrgyzstan: Red, with a gold sun in the middle and a red ring crossed by lines at its center, like a tent roof. Kazakhstan's sun is on sky blue, over an eagle.

## Step 4: The flags with a rug stripe and a cross

These two have a mark that no other flag here has.

The flags, each about 64 pixels wide with its name beside it: 🇹🇲 Turkmenistan; 🇬🇪 Georgia.

Turkmenistan: Green, with an upright red stripe at the left holding five rug patterns, and a white crescent and five stars beside it. No other flag here has a rug stripe.

Georgia: White, with a big red cross that reaches all four edges and a small red cross in each corner. It is the only white flag here.
