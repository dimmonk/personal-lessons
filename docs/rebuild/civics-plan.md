## (a) Every key change, and why (becomes `build.keyChanges`)

Each line is `step | outcome`, was, now, why. The unit that teaches the step or outcome carries its lines.

### The gate (u1)

| Step | Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | "Which part of government is acting, or being asked to act?" (the audited version: "Who has the authority here?", shown as "N1 · ...") | "Who makes the last decision in the case, or is asked to make it?" | K2.2, K2.4. Most cases name two parts of government, and "acting" fitted both. ### Words

- Terms (K6): agency (u1), treaty (u3), executive order (u4), precedent (u5). "federal" is used by the key and is not a term; see gap 2.
- Words to avoid (K9, section 11): franchise, suffrage, ballot (one idea: the right to vote); statute, legislation (one word: law); houses (one word: chambers); void; apportioned; diagnostic; falsify; "who has the authority" (the audited question).
- Factual corrections the key carries: the impeachment answer no longer says "removing"; treaty and conviction thresholds are "two-thirds of the senators present"; the Bill of Rights is part of the Constitution (carried by u2's facts, see below; the old drill's rival options "The Constitution" / "The Bill of Rights" are gone with the drill).

## (b) The unit plan

Ten units. Unit ids are the course order. Gate unit first (A15); branch units in the gate's order; fact units placed after the units whose names their facts may print.

**Ids and the transition.** `subject.units` lists only `u1` to `u7` today. While no unit is rebuilt, `app/state.js` matches the subject's unit ids to the old course by position, and the one-time progress migration (`records.js`, E8) has keyed any stored civics progress as `u1`..`u7` by old position; ten ids would leave three positions with no old unit and stop the app loading. Validator V0 needs every unit the key names (u1, u3, u4, u5, u6) to be in the list, which this satisfies. Once one unit is rebuilt, `state.js` looks old units up by an `id` on each old course entry, and every listed id must be either rebuilt or an old entry. So the commit that rebuilds Unit One must also (1) give each old course entry an id and (2) list the rebuilt units plus the old units still shown, in course order. Two things constrain the old ids. Stored progress (`pl:civics:seen`) is keyed `u1`..`u7` by old position, so an old entry that changes id loses its done mark; and an old entry may not keep an id that a planned unit has taken once that unit is rebuilt. With sequential ids, u4 and u5 (President, courts) take the ids of old Four and Five (rights, history), whose replacements are u8, u9 and u10; u6 and u7 take the ids of old Six and Seven, whose material goes into the branch units. So when u4 or u5 is rebuilt, the old entry holding that id must move to another id (losing its done mark) unless its replacement is rebuilt in the same commit. The order of rebuilding settles which; it is decided in the commit that rebuilds Unit One. Every rebuilt unit is started again under E8 whatever its id, so an id meeting different old content costs only the "Rebuilt: start again" label. This is gap 1.
